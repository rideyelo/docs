A driver sees earnings in four places, and each one is computed differently. The earnings hub reads Stripe, plus completed rides whose fare isn't captured yet. The weekly headline, the status poll, and drive history read `Rides` from Postgres and report net earnings: `driver_payout` less Stripe's processing fee. This page explains where each number comes from and why they disagree.

For payout mechanics (instant fee, idempotency key, payout schedule, Stripe fee backfill), see [Payouts and earnings internals](/engineering/payments/payouts-and-earnings). For how tips are charged, see [Ride tip charging](/engineering/payments/ride-tips). For the product summary, see [Tips and earnings](/concepts/tips-and-earnings).

## How `driver_payout` is built

`Rides.driver_payout` is the gross amount (fare plus tip, in dollars, `real`) that the rider was charged. It's never net of Stripe fees. Driver-facing reads don't show it directly. They pass it to `ride_driver_earnings_cents`, which subtracts the fees (see [Net earnings after Stripe fees](#net-earnings-after-stripe-fees)). Several writers touch it over a ride's life, and most of them also snapshot a fee estimate:

| Moment | Writer | Value | Fee estimate written |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Driver completes ride | `CompleteRideForRating`, from the `complete` ride operation | `GetEffectivePrice()`: `discounted_price` if set, else `price` | `estimated_stripe_fee_cents` |
| Rider tips within the $5.00 buffer | `CompleteRideTip`, from a rider `settle_tip` ride operation | `fare + tip`, status `complete` | `estimated_stripe_fee_cents` (unchanged by the tip) |
| Rider rates with no tip, or tip window expires | `CompleteRideTip` with tip 0, from a rider or system `settle_tip` operation | `fare`, status `complete` | `estimated_stripe_fee_cents` |
| Tip over the buffer, after the fare settled | `RecordCompletedRideTip`, from a `tip` ride operation | `COALESCE(driver_payout, 0) + tip`, only when `tip_amount = 0` | `estimated_tip_stripe_fee_cents` on the tip alone |
| `payment_intent.succeeded` for `payment_intent_id` | `MarkRidePaymentSucceeded` | `amount_received / 100`, skipped when a `RideOperations` row exists. Every ride accepted through a ride operation has one, so this write only reaches older rides | `estimated_stripe_fee_cents`, re-estimated on the fare (plus the $5.00 buffer when tips are enabled) |
| Cancellation with a fee | `CancelReservedRide`, from the `cancel` ride operation | Captured fee amount | `estimated_stripe_fee_cents` on the fee (`stripe_fee_estimate_cents(fee_cents)`) |
| Declined authorization | `CancelDeclinedRideAuthorization`, from a failed `accept` or `promotion` operation | `0` | None |

### Worked example

A $12.00 ride with tips enabled:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete">
    `driver_payout = 12.00`. `tip_window_expires_at` is 24 hours later. Status is `pending_rider_rating`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rider tips $3.00">
    $3.00 is within the $5.00 buffer. One capture of 1500 cents runs. `driver_payout = 15.00`, `tip_amount = 3.00`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Webhook">
    `payment_intent.succeeded` arrives with `amount_received = 1500`. `driver_payout` is written again as `15.00`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

With an $8.00 tip instead, the fare is captured alone (1200 cents), then a separate tip PaymentIntent charges 800 cents. `RecordCompletedRideTip` sets `driver_payout = 20.00`. If the fare's `payment_intent.succeeded` lands afterward, it writes `12.00` back. The tip survives in `tip_amount`, but every sum below loses $8.00. [Stripe webhooks](/engineering/payments/webhooks) documents this race.

## Net earnings after Stripe fees

Stripe takes its processing fee out of the driver's connected account, so what the driver keeps is less than what the rider paid. Every driver-facing read returns this net figure as `driver_earnings` (dollars) or `earnings` (cents). Rider-facing reads keep returning the price the rider paid and never see the fee.

Two Postgres functions, added in `000348` and revised in `000352`, hold the whole definition:

| Function | Returns |
| --- | --- |
| `stripe_fee_estimate_cents(amount_cents)` | `ROUND(amount_cents * 0.029) + 30` for a positive amount, else 0. This is Stripe's standard 2.9% + 30¢ card rate, and the only place the rate lives. Change it with `CREATE OR REPLACE` in a new migration. Stored estimates and actual fees on existing rides aren't affected. |
| `ride_fare_fee_estimate_cents(fare_cents, tips_enabled)` | The fee on the fare charge. Stripe assesses its fee on the amount authorized, not the amount captured, and keeps it when the unused hold is released. A tips-enabled ride authorizes fare + $5.00 (`models.TipBuffer`), so the estimate is `stripe_fee_estimate_cents(fare_cents + 500)`. Otherwise it's the fee on the fare. |
| `ride_driver_earnings_cents(driver_payout, price, discounted_price, tip_amount, stripe_fee_cents, estimated_stripe_fee_cents, tip_stripe_fee_cents, estimated_tip_stripe_fee_cents, tips_enabled)` | Gross less fees, floored at 0. Gross is `driver_payout`, or `COALESCE(discounted_price, price) + tip_amount` before a payout is written. |

`ride_driver_earnings_cents` subtracts one fee per charge, and the actual fee always wins:

1. **Fare charge**: `stripe_fee_cents` (actual, from the fee backfill), else `estimated_stripe_fee_cents` (snapshotted by the payout writers), else a live `ride_fare_fee_estimate_cents`. The live estimate covers rides with no payout yet, such as a request card on the board.
2. **Dedicated tip charge**: `tip_stripe_fee_cents` (actual), else `estimated_tip_stripe_fee_cents`, else 0. Only over-buffer tips charged on their own PaymentIntent have this fee.

<Note>
`000348` also created an 8-argument form of `ride_driver_earnings_cents` that estimates on the captured amount. `000352` adds the 9-argument form with `tips_enabled` and keeps the old one for API instances still running the previous release. Every current query calls the 9-argument form.
</Note>

### Worked example

A $12.00 ride with tips enabled. The fare authorization is 1700 cents (fare plus the $5.00 buffer), so the fare charge's estimated fee is `ROUND(1700 * 0.029) + 30 = 79` cents.

| Scenario | Gross | Fees subtracted | `driver_earnings` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before the rider tips (`pending_rider_rating`) | 1200 | 79 (fare estimate) | $11.21 |
| Rider tips $3.00 inside the buffer | 1500 | 79 (same charge, same authorization) | $14.21 |
| Rider tips $8.00 as a dedicated charge | 2000 | 79 (fare) + 53 (`ROUND(800 * 0.029) + 30`) | $18.68 |

When the fee backfill later stores Stripe's actual fee, the actual replaces the estimate for that charge and the driver's number updates on the next read.

### Rider name on driver-facing reads

Driver-facing reads show the rider's first name and last initial only, for example `Elle A.`. `models.RiderNameForDriver` shortens the `rider_name` string on drive history, activity rows, and pending rides. `models.RiderLastInitial` shortens `rider.last_name` on `GET /ride/i/{id}` for any viewer except the rider, and `rider_last_name` on the driver's message threads. The rider still sees their full name.

## Earnings surfaces

| Surface | Source | Window | Time column | Includes fleet-paid rides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hub balance | Stripe `Balance.Get` on `Users.stripe_merchant_id` | Live | n/a | No |
| Hub `uncaptured` | `ListDriverUncapturedRides`, net | Live | n/a | No, only rides on the driver's account |
| Activity rows | Stripe balance transactions, plus pending rows on the first page | Caller's `from` and `to` | Stripe `created`, or `complete_time` for pending rows | No |
| Activity `gross` and `earnings` per row | `Rides.driver_payout` and `ride_driver_earnings_cents` | Per ride | n/a | No, only rows on the driver's account |
| Weekly headline (`week_total`, `ride_count`) | `GetDriverWeekEarnings` | Monday 00:00 UTC to now | `complete_time` | Yes |
| Status `daily_earnings` | `GetUserDriverStatus` | Rolling 24 hours | `request_time` | Yes |
| Status `weekly_earnings` | `GetUserDriverStatus` | Rolling 7 days | `request_time` | Yes |
| Status `all_time_earnings` | `GetDriverAllTimeEarnings`, Redis 60 s | All time | n/a | Yes |
| Drive history | `GetRideHistoryByDriverID` | Last 200 rides | `request_time` order | Yes |

All database-backed sums add up `ride_driver_earnings_cents` per ride, so they're net of Stripe fees. They filter `status IN ('complete', 'pending_rider_rating')` and `driver_payout IS NOT NULL`. A sum with no rows is returned as `null` (status poll) or `0` (weekly headline).

### Period bucketing and timezones

Every window is UTC or rolling. None uses the community's timezone.

- **Week start.** `startOfWeek` in `mappers.go` computes `offset = (weekday + 6) % 7` and truncates to midnight UTC. Sunday maps to offset 6, so the week starts Monday.
- **Activity groups.** `buildActivityPage` groups by `time.Unix(created).UTC().Format("2006-01-02")`. The `date_key` is a UTC date.
- **Status poll.** `request_time > CURRENT_TIMESTAMP - INTERVAL '1 day'` and `'1 week'`. These are rolling and keyed on request time, not completion.

Worked example for a driver in `America/New_York` during EDT (UTC-4):

| Ride | Requested (local) | Completed (local) | Completed (UTC) | Headline week | Activity `date_key` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Sun 7:30 pm | Sun 7:50 pm | Sun 23:50 | Previous week | Sunday |
| B | Sun 8:30 pm | Sun 8:50 pm | Mon 00:50 | Current week | Monday |

On Monday morning, the headline includes ride B but not ride A. The hub labels ride B as Monday, although the driver drove it Sunday night. The status poll's `weekly_earnings` includes both, because it's a rolling 7 days.

The mobile client picks its `from` in UTC too. `useEarningsHubData` sends `from = start of today UTC - 7 days` with `limit = 25`. `useAllActivity` sends `from = Monday 00:00 UTC - 30 days` with `limit = 100`.

## Activity feed

`GET /earnings/activity` runs in this order (`Service.GetActivity`):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Resolve the account">
    `getMerchantID` returns the dev override or `Users.stripe_merchant_id`. See [account resolution](/engineering/payments/payouts-and-earnings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="List transactions">
    `ListBalanceTransactions` sends `limit` (`normalizeLimit`: default 25, max 100), `starting_after = cursor`, and `created[gte]` and `created[lte]` from `from` and `to`. A zero or unparsable `from` or `to` is dropped (`parseInt64Query` returns 0, `buildCreatedRange` skips values of 0 or less).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the page">
    `buildActivityPage` maps each transaction, classifies its type, appends it to its UTC day group, and adds `net` to that group's `total`. It also sums a page-local week total from rows with `created` on or after the week start and `net` greater than 0. Every `charge` or `payment` row among those counts as a ride.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enrich with rides">
    `enrichActivityWithRides` reads `source.charge.payment_intent.id` from each transaction and runs one `GetRideEarningsByPaymentIntents` query (`WHERE payment_intent_id = ANY($1)`). Matched rows get `ride_id`, `rider_name` (first name and last initial), `tip_amount` (cents, `math.Round(tip * 100)`), `gross` (cents from `driver_payout`), and `earnings` (cents from `ride_driver_earnings_cents`). A query failure logs a warning and returns bare rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add pending rides">
    When `cursor` is empty, `addPendingRides` merges the driver's uncaptured rides into the page as pending rows. See [Pending rides](#pending-uncaptured-rides). A failed lookup logs a warning and adds nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Replace the headline">
    When `cursor` is empty, `weeklySummary` overwrites `week_total` and `ride_count` with `GetDriverWeekEarnings`. A failure logs a warning and keeps the page-local values.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Row fields

| Field | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | Balance transaction ID, or `pending_{rideID}` for a pending row | Used as the cursor |
| `type` | `classifyTransactionType` | `charge`, `payment`, `payout`, `refund`, `adjustment`, `fee`, `other` |
| `amount`, `net`, `fee` | Stripe, cents | Stripe books the charge at the authorized amount, per the `weeklySummary` comment |
| `gross` | `Rides.driver_payout` | Omitted when 0 or unmatched |
| `earnings` | `ride_driver_earnings_cents` | What the driver keeps, in cents. Omitted when 0 or unmatched. The client shows `earnings` when present, then `gross`, then `net` (`mappers.ts`) |
| `pending` | `addPendingRides` | `true` on rows for rides whose fare isn't captured yet. Omitted otherwise. `net` and `fee` on these rows are estimates |
| `tip_amount` | `Rides.tip_amount` | Cents |
| `created_at`, `available_on` | Stripe | Unix seconds |

### Headline math

`GetDriverWeekEarnings` sums `ride_driver_earnings_cents` in Postgres and returns dollars. The repository converts back with `int64(math.Round(total * 100))`.

Example week, with no tips enabled and estimated fees only: rides with `driver_payout` of 12.00 (fee 65¢), 15.00 (fee 74¢), and 7.50 (fee 52¢), plus a canceled ride with a $3.00 fee (fee 39¢). The query returns `total_dollars = 35.20` and `ride_count = 4`. The response carries `week_total = 3520` and `ride_count = 4`. The canceled ride counts as a ride because the query doesn't filter `cancel_phase`.

### Pagination

| List | Cursor returned | Page size | `has_more` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Activity | ID of the last raw transaction (`page.Data[len-1]`) | `normalizeLimit` | Stripe's `has_more` for the fetched list |
| Payouts | ID of the last mapped payout | `normalizeLimit` | Same |

Both Stripe helpers stop iterating once they've collected `Limit` items, so they never auto-page past one request. Pass `next_cursor` back as `cursor` to read older rows. The cursor is only valid with the same `from` and `to`.

<Note>
The mobile app never sends `cursor`. The hub and the **All activity** screen each read one page, so rows beyond 25 or 100 in the window aren't shown.
</Note>

## Status poll earnings

`UserRepository.GetStatus` runs `GetUserDriverStatus` on every `GET /user/status` poll. The query's `recent_drives` CTE is bounded to one week of `request_time`, so daily and weekly sums stay cheap. Daily, weekly, and all-time sums are net of Stripe fees: each ride contributes `ride_driver_earnings_cents / 100`, not `driver_payout`.

All-time earnings used to run inside the same query. They're now served by `DriverEarningsCache` (`internal/data/redis/driver_status_cache.go`):

- Key `driver:earnings:all_time:{driverID}`, TTL 60 seconds.
- The cached value wraps the sum as `{"sum": ...}`, so a driver with no rides (null) is still a cache hit.
- An undecodable value is deleted and recomputed.
- With Redis disabled, the loader runs inline on every poll.
- Nothing calls `Invalidate`. A completed ride can take up to 60 seconds to reach `all_time_earnings`.

## Drive history

`GET /user/drive-history` calls `GetRideHistoryByDriverID`. It returns up to 200 rides with status `complete` or `pending_rider_rating`, newest `request_time` first.

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `price` | `COALESCE(discounted_price, price)`, the fare. Not `driver_payout` |
| `tip_amount` | `COALESCE(tip_amount, 0)` |
| `fee_cents` | Gross less `driver_earnings`, in cents. This is the fee applied to the driver's earnings across the fare charge and any dedicated tip charge: actual where Stripe has reported it, else the estimate. Never null on drive history |
| `driver_earnings` | `ride_driver_earnings_cents / 100`, what the driver keeps, in dollars |
| `pending` | `true` while the fare is authorized but not captured. `driver_earnings` isn't in the Stripe balance yet, `fee_cents` is an estimate, and a tip may still be added |
| `rider_name` | Rider first name and last initial |
| `duration` | Seconds from `request_time` to `complete_time` |

Gross less `fee_cents` always equals `driver_earnings`. The history query doesn't filter `cancel_phase`, so canceled rides appear with their full quoted fare in `price`.

`GET /user/ride-history` (the rider's own history) no longer returns a fee. Its `fee_cents` is always null and it has no `driver_earnings`.

## Pending (uncaptured) rides

On a tips-enabled ride, the fare stays authorized but uncaptured during the rider's 24-hour tip window. Stripe has no balance transaction for it yet, so the Stripe balance and activity list leave out a ride the driver just finished. The API reports these rides from Postgres instead.

`ListDriverUncapturedRides` selects up to 100 of the driver's rides on the given connected account where:

- `status = 'pending_rider_rating'` and `cancel_phase IS NULL`,
- `tips_enabled = TRUE`, `payment_intent_id IS NOT NULL`, and `driver_payout > 0`,
- `complete_time` is set, and
- `tip_window_expires_at > NOW() - INTERVAL '1 hour'`.

Rides more than an hour past their window are stuck captures awaiting manual review. They're left out instead of being promised to the driver. Drive history's `pending` flag uses the same rule.

Pending rides show up in three places:

| Surface | Field | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /earnings/balance` | `uncaptured`, `uncaptured_rides` | Sum of the pending rides' net earnings in cents, and their count. Not part of `available`, `pending`, or `instant_available`, and can't be paid out |
| `GET /earnings/activity`, first page | Rows with `pending: true` | One `charge` row per ride: `amount` and `gross` are the gross, `earnings` and `net` are the net, `fee` is the difference, `created_at` is `complete_time`. Added to its UTC day group's `total` |
| `GET /user/drive-history` | `pending` | `true` on the ride's record |

Example `data` payload from `GET /earnings/balance` for a driver with two rides in their tip windows:

```json
{
  "available": 4210,
  "pending": 1530,
  "instant_available": 4210,
  "currency": "usd",
  "uncaptured": 2363,
  "uncaptured_rides": 2
}
```

The lookup is best-effort. A failure logs `[earnings] uncaptured rides lookup failed; serving Stripe balance only` and returns `uncaptured: 0`.

When the rider rates the ride (with or without a tip), `RideService.Rate` sends the driver a ride-status ping with status `complete`, so the driver's app refreshes its balance and history right away.

## Payout pushes

`HandlePayoutEvent` (`internal/service/earnings/webhook.go`) runs for `payout.paid`, `payout.failed`, and `payout.canceled`:

1. A nil payout returns 400.
2. An empty `event.account` (platform payout) is ignored.
3. `GetByStripeMerchantId` loads the user that owns the connected account.
4. `formatUSD` renders the absolute amount, for example `12345` becomes `$123.45`.
5. `NotifyPayout` emits `payout_paid`, `payout_failed`, or `payout_canceled` with event key `{eventType}:{eventID}` and data `payout_id`, `event_type`, `amount`, `currency`, `arrival`, and `deeplink = yelo://drive/earnings`.
6. A notification error is logged, and the webhook still returns 200.

Copy comes from the seeded templates in `internal/notify/seed/templates.go`, for example **Payout deposited**: "`amount` just landed in your bank account." See the [notification catalog](/engineering/notifications/catalog). Delivery and retry semantics are on [Stripe webhooks](/engineering/payments/webhooks).

## Demand dashboard

`GET /driver/demand-dashboard` reads the caller's community from the JWT context.

- `demand_day` is an integer from 0 to 6 (Sunday is 0). The OpenAPI option marks it required, but the handler accepts an empty value.
- With no `demand_day`, the handler uses `time.Now()` in `America/New_York`, minus 6 hours, and takes its weekday. At 2:00 am Saturday Eastern, the default day is still Friday.
- `ride_requests_last_20_minutes` counts rides in the community with `confirm_time` in the last 20 minutes.
- `drivers_online` counts open `DriverSessions` whose driver's current `community_id` matches.
- The curve is the newest `RideDemandCurves` row for the day with `end_time` in the future. None returns empty arrays.
- `ToResponse` converts each `x` (hours) to a timestamp from midnight Eastern of the shifted "today", regardless of `demand_day`.

## Auto-queue settings and uptime log

The matching side of auto-queue is on [Auto-queue scoring](/engineering/matching/auto-queue-scoring). This section covers the driver's own settings endpoints.

### Settings

`GetAutoQueueSettings` returns:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `enabled` | `Users.auto_queue` |
| `max_minutes` | `Users.auto_queue_max_minutes`, null for community default |
| `effective_max_minutes` | `min(max_minutes or community default, community max)` |
| `community_max_minutes`, `community_default_minutes` | `Communities` columns |
| `paused_until` | `Users.auto_queue_paused_until`, or null when it's in the past |

Example: a community with max 60 and default 30. A driver with `max_minutes = null` gets 30. A driver with `max_minutes = 45` gets 45. If the community later drops its max to 40, that driver gets 40 without an error.

`SetAutoQueue` rejects `max_minutes` above the community max ("Your max minutes cannot exceed the community limit of N minutes."). It then calls `UpdateAutoQueueSettings` and sets the Redis presence flag with `SetAutoQueueFlag`. A presence failure is logged, not returned.

`PauseAutoQueue` accepts `duration_minutes` from 0 to 60 (`maxPauseMinutes`). A positive value sets `auto_queue_paused_until = now + duration`. Zero clears it. The response is the refreshed settings.

### Uptime accounting

`reconcileDriverAutoQueueAccounting` (`internal/data/repository/auto_queue_settings.go`) keeps `DriverAutoQueueLog` in step with the server's state. It runs inside `withDriverSessionLock`, in the same transaction as the state change, from four writers:

- `UpdateAutoQueueSettings` (settings command)
- `SetDriverOnline` with `false` (going offline)
- `RefreshDriverSession` (going online)
- `SetInactiveDriversOffline` (stale-driver expiry)

The rule is one line: if `is_online_driver AND auto_queue`, open an interval, otherwise close it. `OpenDriverAutoQueueInterval` inserts only when no open row exists, so repeated commands don't split intervals. `CloseDriverAutoQueueInterval` sets `disabled_at` on every open row.

Pausing doesn't touch the log. A driver paused for 30 minutes keeps accruing uptime.

`SumUptimeDuringOperatingHours` reads the log for reporting:

- Each interval ends at the earliest of `disabled_at` (or now), `enabled_at + 12h` (`AutoQueueMaxSessionCap`), and the window end.
- Overlap with operating hours is counted minute by minute in the community's timezone (`operatingOverlapSeconds`). Without configured windows, the default is 19:00 to 03:00 every day.
- Timezone changes split the interval, so a correction doesn't reinterpret past uptime.
- Intervals whose community was deleted (`community_id` null) are excluded and logged.

Example: a driver enables auto-queue at 18:30 local and goes offline at 20:15, with the default window. Only 19:00 to 20:15 counts: 75 minutes, or 4500 seconds.

## Gotchas and edge cases

- **`tip_count` is always 0.** `buildActivityPage` declares `tipCount` and never increments it. The response field exists, but carries no data.
- **Page-local totals on later pages.** Only the first page (empty `cursor`) gets the database headline. A request with a cursor returns `week_total` and `ride_count` computed from that page's rows alone.
- **Headline counts canceled rides.** `GetDriverWeekEarnings` and the status poll don't filter `cancel_phase`, so a cancellation fee counts as a ride. `drive_count` on the user profile does filter it.
- **Over-buffer tips can show twice.** Enrichment matches only `Rides.payment_intent_id`. The fare row gets `gross` and `earnings` that include the tip, and the dedicated tip PaymentIntent's row stays unenriched with its own `net`. The client shows both.
- **Estimates can drift until the backfill runs.** A ride shows the estimated fee until `stripe_drive_fee_repair` stores Stripe's actual fee, which can shift `driver_earnings` by a few cents. Rides from before `000348` have no stored estimate and use the live estimate.
- **Hub total versus cash-out.** The app's hub total and pending amount include `uncaptured`. The next payout and the cash-out amount use only Stripe's buckets.
- **Headline versus status weekly.** The headline is a calendar week from Monday 00:00 UTC on `complete_time`. `weekly_earnings` is a rolling 7 days on `request_time`. They rarely match.
- **Fleet-paid rides.** Database sums include rides charged on a fleet account. Stripe-backed balance, activity, and payouts don't. See [payout routing](/engineering/fleets/membership-and-payout-routing).
- **Dev override.** Outside `dev`, `development`, `local`, and `test`, `EARNINGS_DEV_OVERRIDE_MERCHANT_ID` is ignored. Inside them, every user's Stripe calls hit that account, but the weekly headline still reads the caller's own rides.
- **Unknown connected accounts fail the webhook.** `GetUserProfileByStripeMerchantID` is a `:one` query, so no match returns an error, not nil. The `user == nil` branch in `HandlePayoutEvent` never runs, and payouts on fleet accounts return 500.
- **Negative `max_minutes`.** `SetAutoQueue` only checks the upper bound. A negative or zero value is stored and becomes `effective_max_minutes`.
- **Pause and uptime.** Pausing leaves the uptime interval open, so paused time counts as auto-queue uptime.
- **Demand curve anchor.** The curve's timestamps always use the shifted "today" in Eastern time, even when `demand_day` asks for another day.
- **Monthly schedule estimate.** `nextMonthlyAnchor` uses `time.Date`, which normalizes overflow. An anchor of 31 in April estimates May 1.
