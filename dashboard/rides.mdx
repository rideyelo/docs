The **Rides** page is where you monitor trips and ride problems. It has four tabs:

- **KPIs**: ride volume, completion rate, and cancel and uncomplete rates for one market.
- **Trips**: a live, filterable list of rides, with a ride map for admins.
- **Issues**: post-match cancellations and uncompleted rides for one market.
- **Searches**: rider search analytics and top locations for one market.

For metric definitions, see [Ride metrics](/dashboard/ride-metrics). For how a ride moves through its states, see [Ride lifecycle](/concepts/ride-lifecycle).

## Prerequisites

- An admin, intern, or fleet manager account. See [Dashboard roles and access](/administration/dashboard-roles).

| Role | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Admin | All four tabs. You can view all markets at once on **Trips** and open ride details. |
| Intern | All four tabs for your assigned community. |
| Fleet manager | Only **Trips**, limited to rides in your assigned fleets. |

The selected tab is saved in the page URL, so you can share a link to a specific tab.

## Choose a market

Admins pick a market with the community selector in the sidebar, or with the market menu on the **Trips** map. The selected market is saved in the page URL as `?community=`. When no market is selected, **Trips** shows **All markets**.

**KPIs**, **Issues**, and **Searches** only work for one market. If no market is selected, they show **Choose a market to explore**. Select a market from the menu to continue.

Interns always see their assigned community. For fleet managers, the sidebar shows the current fleet and community. Fleet managers with assignments in more than one community switch communities by searching for the community name in the global search.

## Trips tab

**Trips** lists rides, newest first. The list refreshes every 10 seconds while any ride is active, and every 60 seconds otherwise. The **Updated** label shows when it last refreshed. Click **Refresh** to reload immediately.

### Ride map

Admins see a ride map above the list. It draws the stored quoted path for each ride on the current page of results. These are planned routes, not GPS traces.

1. Pick a market from the market menu, or leave it on **All markets**.
2. Click the locate button to fit the map to the market.
3. Open the layer panel in the top-left corner of the map and turn layers on or off: **Ride paths**, **Searches**, **Market boundaries**, and **Zones**. Zones only appear when a market is selected. The panel also shows the color legend.
4. Click a path to open that ride's details.

Paths and table rows are colored by outcome:

| Legend | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Matched / completed** | A driver matched the ride, whether it's in progress or complete. |
| **Awaiting match** | The rider confirmed and the ride is waiting for a driver. |
| **Unsuccessful match** | No driver was found, or the ride was canceled before a match. |
| **Canceled after match** | The ride was canceled after a driver matched. |
| **Searches / superseded** | A search, or a request replaced by a newer search from the same rider. |

### Filter the list

- **Status**: Click **All**, **Active**, **Completed**, or **Canceled**.
- **Search**: Type in **Search rider, driver or ride ID...**.
- **Fleet**: Pick a fleet from **All Fleets**. This filter isn't shown to fleet managers.
- **Columns**: Click **Columns** to show or hide columns.

Rides older than 24 hours are collapsed. Click **Show N older rides (24hr+)** to include them, and **Hide older rides** to collapse them again. The list shows 50 rides per page.

### Trips columns

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Community** | The ride's community. Only shown when viewing all markets. |
| **Rider** | The rider. Click the name to open the user's details. |
| **Driver** | The assigned driver, if any. |
| **Status** | The ride's current status. |
| **When** | Time since the request. Hover for the exact time. |
| **TTM** | Time to match: from confirm to driver accept. |
| **TTP** | Time to pickup: from match to driver arrival. |
| **TTD** | Time to dropoff: from pickup to dropoff. |
| **Wait** | Total rider wait: from confirm to pickup. |
| **Time** | Rider time, with driver time in parentheses, for the full ride. |
| **Distance** | Ride distance, with pickup distance. |
| **Fare** | The ride price. |
| **Pickup** | Pickup location name. |
| **Dropoff** | Dropoff location name. |
| **Fleet** | The fleet that served the ride. |
| **Rating** | For completed rides, the driver rating (**D**) and rider rating (**R**). |

### Open ride details

Admins can click any ride row or map path to open **Ride Details**. The panel shows the ride's route map and a **Ride Timeline**.

The timeline summary shows **TTM**, **TTP**, **Duration**, **Declines**, and **Events**. The event table lists each event with its **Time**, **Elapsed**, **Event**, **Actor**, and **Transition**.

**Ride Details** opens the same way from the **Issues** tab, the rides table on the [Live](/dashboard/live#monitor-rides) page, and recent pricing activity on **Pricing Management**.

### Cancel a ride silently

Admins can cancel a stuck ride without notifying the rider or driver. Use it when a ride is still open in Yelo but the trip is clearly over or never happened, for example when the driver never recorded the pickup.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ride">
    Open **Ride Details** for the ride. The **Admin cancellation** section appears below the **Ride Timeline**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check eligibility">
    Yelo checks whether the ride can be canceled. If it can't, the section explains why and **Cancel ride silently** stays disabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a reason">
    Click **Cancel ride silently**. In the **Cancel this ride silently?** dialog, enter a **Reason for cancellation** of up to 500 characters. The reason is saved in the ride's audit event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm silent cancellation**, or **Keep ride** to back out. The dashboard shows **Ride canceled. No notifications sent.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

A ride can be canceled only when all of these are true. Otherwise the section shows the reason it's blocked.

| Requirement | Message when it isn't met |
| --- | --- |
| The ride is **Driver En Route**, **Driver Waiting**, or **In Progress**. | Only an active, accepted ride can be canceled here. |
| The fare is $0 and the ride has no payment or tip payment. | This ride requires payment review before it can be canceled. |
| No ride operation is pending or quarantined. | A ride operation is unresolved. Resolve it before canceling. |
| The driver has no other queued ride. | This driver has a queued ride that must be handled before canceling. |
| No notification for the ride is queued or sending. | A notification is already queued or sending. Wait for it to finish before canceling. |

Completed rides and rides waiting for a rating don't show the section.

When you confirm, Yelo:

- Ends the ride as a system cancellation, with the ride's previous status as the cancel phase and `admin_silent_cancellation` as the cancel reason.
- Adds a `ride_canceled` event to the timeline, with your admin ID and reason.
- Doesn't charge anyone, send push notifications, texts, or emails, or move the driver's next queued ride forward.

The rider's and driver's apps show the change the next time they refresh. If the ride changed after you opened the dialog, Yelo rejects the cancellation without changing anything. Close the dialog and try again.

<Warning>
  You can't undo a silent cancellation from the dashboard. The ride counts as a system cancellation after a match in ride metrics.
</Warning>

## KPIs tab

**KPIs** summarizes ride performance for one market. Pick **Last 24 Hours**, **Last 7 Days**, **Last 14 Days**, **Last 30 Days**, or **All Time** with the range menu. The default is **Last 30 Days**. Each card compares the range with the prior period of the same length.

| Section | Cards |
| --- | --- |
| **Ride Volume** | **Ride Requests**, **Confirmed Requests**, **Completed Rides**, **Cancelled Rides**, **Uncompleted Rides** |
| **Ride requests over time** | Hourly request volume in the market's time zone. |
| **Rates** | **Completion Rate**, **Driver Cancel Rate**, **Rider Cancel Rate**, **System Cancel Rate** |
| **Incomplete Rides** | **Driver Uncomplete Rate**, **Rider Uncomplete Rate**, **System Uncomplete Rate**, **No Drivers Available** |

The cancel rates are shares of post-match cancellations, not shares of all rides. For example, a **Driver Cancel Rate** of 40% means drivers caused 40% of cancellations. The uncomplete rates work the same way against total uncompletes. The system share is whatever isn't attributed to the rider or the driver.

## Issues tab

**Issues** shows two reports for one market, stacked on one tab. One range menu at the top controls both, with the same options as **KPIs**. The default is **Last 7 Days**. The range is saved in the page URL as `?range=`, so links from the **Live** page open **Issues** with the same period.

Click any row in either table to open **Ride Details** for that ride. Click a rider or driver name to open the user's details.

### Post-match cancellations

This report covers rides canceled after a driver matched. See [Cancellations](/concepts/cancellations).

| Card | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Cancellations** | Post-match cancellations in the range. |
| **Avg Time After Match** | Average time between the match and the cancellation. |
| **Avg Time to Cancel** | Average time between the request and the cancellation. |
| **System Rate**, **Driver Rate**, **Rider Rate** | Share of post-match cancellations caused by the system, the driver, or the rider. |

The **By Reason** and **By Phase** charts break cancellations down by cancel reason and by the ride state at cancellation.

The **Recent Cancellations** table has these columns: **Cancel Time**, **Ride ID**, **Canceled By**, **Phase**, **Reason**, **Rider**, **Driver**, and **Time to Cancel**. Click **Load more** to see older rows.

### Uncompletes

This report covers confirmed rides that never matched with a driver.

| Card | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Uncompletes** | Uncompleted rides in the range. |
| **Avg Time to Uncomplete** | Average time from request until the ride ended. |
| **Avg Stale Age** | Average age of rides stuck in flight. |
| **System Rate**, **Driver Rate**, **Rider Rate** | Share of uncompletes caused by the system, the driver, or the rider. |
| **No Drivers Available** | Share of uncompletes where no driver was found. |
| **Stale In-Flight** | Share of uncompletes still in flight without a driver. |

The **By Category** and **By Status** charts break uncompletes down by outcome and final status. For each category, see [Uncompleted ride outcomes](/dashboard/ride-metrics#uncompleted-ride-outcomes).

The **Recent Uncompletes** table has these columns: **End Time**, **Ride ID**, **Category**, **Status**, **Rider**, **Driver**, and **Time to End**.

## Searches tab

**Searches** shows how riders search in one market. It has two sub-tabs. Pick a range at the top. The default is **Last 30 Days**.

<Note>
  Admins and interns can also open this view directly at `/searches`.
</Note>

### Search Analytics

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Searches**, **Total Rides**, **Overall Conversion Rate** | Search volume, resulting rides, and the share of searches that became rides. |
| **Search Funnel** | Counts for **Browse**, **Select**, **Request**, and **Complete**, with the conversion rate between each step. |
| **Search Activity Map** | A heatmap of search locations inside the market boundary. |
| **Top Searched Locations** | Each searched **Location** with its **Searches**, **Rides**, and **Conversion Rate**. |
| **Recent Searches** | The latest searches, with **Time**, **Rider**, **Address**, **Type**, and **Coordinates**. |

In **Recent Searches**, **Type** is **No results** when the search returned nothing, and **Result** otherwise.

### Top Locations

Use **Top Locations** to manage the featured places riders see when they open search. See [Community top locations](/guides/community-top-locations).
