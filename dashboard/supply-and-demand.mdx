The **Supply & Demand** page is the live operations view for every market. Use it to spot markets with waiting riders, get drivers online, and set temporary surge pricing.

The page has three tabs:

- **Overview**: 24-hour KPIs and a live card for each market. This page covers this tab.
- **Presence**: who has the app open right now. See [Presence](/dashboard/presence).
- **Driver Ops**: a per-driver view of queues, balances, and activity. See [Driver ops](/dashboard/driver-ops).

## Prerequisites

- An admin account. See [Dashboard roles and access](/administration/dashboard-roles).

## Read the 24-hour KPIs

The cards at the top cover all markets. Each card shows the last 24 hours and the change from the 24 hours before that. The cards refresh every minute. Test users and test communities are excluded.

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **User Signups** | Users who completed their profile. |
| **Driver Signups** | Drivers whose application was activated. |
| **Completes** | Completed rides that weren't canceled. |
| **GMV** | Sum of fares for completed rides, using the discounted price when one applies. |
| **Incompletes** | Rides that found no driver, were canceled before a driver was assigned, or are stuck in flight for more than an hour. |

## Monitor markets

Below the KPIs, each active community appears as a market card. The list refreshes every 15 seconds, and the header shows when it last updated.

Sort the list with **Activity** (the default) or **A-Z**. **Activity** ranks markets by drivers online plus active rides.

Each market header shows:

| Item | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **LIVE** or **OFF PEAK** | Whether the market is inside its operating hours. See [Operating hours](/admin/operating-hours). |
| **N.Nx surge** | Shown when a surge multiplier above 1.0x is active. |
| **Drivers** | Drivers with an open driving session. |
| **Rides** | Rides that are confirmed, queued, or in progress. |
| **Unmatched** | Requests from the last 24 hours that have no driver, either still waiting or ended with no driver available. Shown in red when above zero. |

Markets with any drivers, rides, or unmatched requests expand automatically. Click a market header to expand or collapse it.

An expanded market shows a control bar, a **Drivers** table, and a **Rides** table.

### Drivers table

The drivers table lists the market's active drivers who are online or were active in the last 7 days. Online drivers appear first, then warm drivers, then offline drivers. It refreshes about every 15 to 20 seconds.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Driver name and user tags. |
| **Status** | **Online**, **Warm**, or **Offline**. |
| **Vehicle** | **X**, **XL**, **Cart**, or **Mini**. |
| **Last Active** | **Active now** for online drivers, otherwise the time since the driver was last seen. |
| **Rides Today** | Completed rides since midnight in the market's time zone. |
| **Fleet** | The driver's fleet. |
| **Decline Reasons** | Reasons for rides the driver declined today. |
| **Actions** | **Ping driver** and **Copy phone**. |

A **Warm** driver isn't online but was active in the matching warm pool within the last hour. Warm drivers are good candidates to ping. See [Matching and auto-queue](/concepts/matching-and-auto-queue).

### Rides table

The rides table shows the market's rides from the last hour. Searches that were never confirmed aren't listed. When older rides exist, turn on **Show older rides** to include the last 24 hours.

Filter with **All**, **Active**, **Completed**, or **Canceled**, or type in **Search rider or driver...**. Click **When**, **TTM**, **TTP**, **TTD**, **Wait**, **Distance**, or **Fare** to sort.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Rider** | The rider. |
| **Driver** | The assigned driver, or **Awaiting match**. |
| **Status** | For example **Confirmed**, **Queued**, **Driver En Route**, **In Progress**, **Complete**, or **Unsuccessful Match**. |
| **When** | Time since the request. |
| **TTM** | Time to match. |
| **TTP** | Time to pickup. |
| **TTD** | Time to dropoff. |
| **Wait** | Total rider wait. |
| **Distance** | Ride distance, with pickup distance. |
| **Pickup** and **Dropoff** | Location names. |
| **Fleet** | The fleet that served the ride. |
| **Fare** | The ride price. |
| **Rating** | The ride's rating. |
| **Cancel / Decline** | Who canceled (**Rider canceled**, **Driver canceled**, **System canceled**, or **Superseded**), the cancel reason, and drivers who declined. |
| **Actions** | **Ping rider**. |

Click a ride row to open **Ride Details** with the route map and ride timeline. See [Rides](/dashboard/rides#open-ride-details).

## Set or clear surge pricing

Surge multiplies the configured fare for new quotes in a market until it expires. It doesn't apply to fallback fares in markets without a pricing config. See [Pricing](/concepts/pricing) and [Ride pricing](/dashboard/ride-pricing).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Move the Surge slider">
    In the market's control bar, drag **Surge** to a value from **1.0x** to **3.0x**, in steps of 0.1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Apply">
    **Apply** appears once the value differs from the current multiplier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a duration and confirm">
    In the **Set Surge** dialog, choose a **Duration** of **1 hour** to **4 hours**. Click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While surge is active, the control bar shows the multiplier and the time left, such as **1.5x surge - 42m left**. Surge ends on its own when the duration runs out.

To end surge early, drag the slider back to **1.0x**, click **Apply**, and confirm in the **Clear Surge** dialog.

<Warning>
  Surge changes rider prices immediately. Yelo records who set each surge, but the dashboard doesn't ask for a reason.
</Warning>

## Notify a whole market

Send one push notification to every driver or every rider in a market.

1. In the market's control bar, click **Notify Drivers** or **Notify Riders**.
2. Edit the **Title** (up to 60 characters) and **Body** (up to 200 characters). Each audience has a default message, such as **Riders are waiting!** for drivers.
3. Click **Send**.

For how push delivery works, see [Notifications](/concepts/notifications).

## Ping one driver or rider

1. In the **Drivers** table, click **Ping driver**. In the **Rides** table, click **Ping rider**.
2. Edit the **Title** and **Message**. The limits are 60 and 200 characters.
3. Click **Send**.

If the dashboard says the notification wasn't sent, the person likely has push notifications turned off. Click **Copy phone** in the **Drivers** table to reach a driver another way.
