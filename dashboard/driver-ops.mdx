title: "Driver ops"
description: "Track every driver's live state, auto-queue, ride queue, and balance by market, and nudge drivers from the Driver Ops tab"
---

The **Driver Ops** tab on the **Supply & Demand** page is a per-driver view of every market. Use it to see which drivers are online or in auto-queue, who is waiting in each driver's queue, and which markets have gone quiet.

Driver Ops shows the same drivers as the market cards on the [Supply and demand](/dashboard/supply-and-demand) overview, with more detail on each one.

## Prerequisites

- An admin account. See [Dashboard roles and access](/administration/dashboard-roles).

## Open Driver Ops

1. Click **Supply & Demand** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **Driver Ops** tab.

The tab refreshes every 20 seconds. **Updated** shows the time of the last refresh. Timers on each driver count up every second between refreshes.

## Read a market

Each market is a collapsed row. The header shows:

| Item | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **N online** | Drivers currently online. |
| **AQ: N** | Drivers with auto-queue turned on. |
| **Founding: N** | Drivers tagged **Founding Driver**. |
| **No activity 7d** | None of the market's drivers has been active in the last 7 days. |
| **Last req** | The most recent ride request: rider name, status, and time since the request. |

Click a market to expand its driver list.

## Read a driver card

Each driver card shows the driver's name, an **AQ** badge when auto-queue is on, and the driver's user tags.

Below the name, a status line shows the driver's current state:

| Status line | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Declined: reason** | The driver declined a ride today. This line takes priority over the other states. |
| **En route**, **Waiting at pickup**, or another ride status | The driver has an active ride, with the time in that state. **En route** means the rider is in the car. |
| **Online — no active ride** | The driver is online and free. |
| **Warm pool** | The driver was recently active in the matching warm pool but isn't online. |
| **Offline · last seen** | The driver is offline, with the time since the last heartbeat. |

The card also shows the driver's **Vehicle**, **Fleet**, **Rides today**, and time since the last **Heartbeat**.

For online drivers, the card also shows:

- **Balance**: the driver's available earnings, with any pending amount.
- **AQ payout**: shown as a dash. Auto-queue payout tracking isn't available yet.
- **Queue**: riders queued to the driver, with how long each has been waiting. Shown when the driver has queued riders or has auto-queue on.

For how auto-queue and driver queues work, see [Matching and auto-queue](/concepts/matching-and-auto-queue). For earnings, see [Payments and payouts](/concepts/payments-and-payouts).

## Find a driver

Type a name in **Search drivers…**. The market list is replaced by **Search results**, which lists matching drivers from every market with the market name above each card.

## Show inactive drivers

By default, the tab hides drivers who aren't online or warm and haven't been seen in the last 14 days. Turn on **Show all (14d+)** to include them.

## Contact drivers

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Ping** | Sends the driver a push notification right away, titled **Hey!**, with the message "Check in when you get a chance." You can't edit this message. |
| **Quo** | Opens a search for the driver's phone number in Quo in a new tab. |
| **Notify all** | In a market header, opens **Notify Drivers** to send one push notification to every driver in the market. |

To send a custom message to one driver, use **Ping driver** on the [Supply and demand](/dashboard/supply-and-demand#ping-one-driver-or-rider) overview instead.
