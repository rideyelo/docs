The **Telemetry** page summarizes the devices that run the Yelo app. Use it to check platform mix, OS and app version adoption, and how many users grant notification, location, and contacts permissions.

## Prerequisites

- An admin account. See [Dashboard roles and access](/administration/dashboard-roles).

## Open telemetry

1. Expand **Administration** in the sidebar and click **Telemetry**.
2. Pick a community from the community filter, or leave it on **All Communities**.

Each device counts once, based on its latest report. Only devices of users with a completed profile are counted. Test users and test communities are excluded.

## Telemetry metrics

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Reporting Devices** | Devices that have sent telemetry. |
| **iOS** | iOS devices, with their share of all devices. |
| **Android** | Android devices, with their share of all devices. |
| **iOS 26 Adoption** | Share of iOS devices on iOS 26. |
| **Contacts Granted** | Share of contacts permission responses that are **Granted**. |

## Charts

| Chart | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Liquid Glass adoption** | iOS 26 devices compared with earlier iOS versions. |
| **OS major versions** | Devices by OS major version. Patch releases are grouped into their major release. |
| **Device type** | Models grouped into phone, tablet, simulator, and unmapped families. |
| **Device generation / family** | Models grouped into readable generations. |
| **Permissions** | Response breakdown for **Notifications**, **Location**, and **Contacts**. |
| **App versions** | Devices by app version, with the most common version in the description. |
| **Platforms** | Devices by platform. |
| **Locales** | Devices by locale. |
| **Screen sizes** | Devices by screen size. |

## Filter permissions

The **Permissions** chart shows each permission as a bar split by state: **Granted**, **While using**, **Limited**, **Denied**, and **Not determined**.

Turn off **Include not determined** to drop **Not determined** responses from the counts and percentages. This also changes the **Contacts Granted** card.

<Note>
  Notifications only report enabled or disabled. The app can't tell whether the user was ever prompted.
</Note>

## Filter app versions

Use the timeframe menu above **App versions** to limit it to devices that reported in the chosen window.

| Option | Devices included |
| --- | --- |
| **Last 1 month** | Devices whose latest report is from the last month. |
| **Last 3 months** | Devices whose latest report is from the last three months. |
| **All time** | Every reporting device. This is the default. |

The timeframe only affects **App versions**. Use **Last 1 month** to see which versions active users are on, without old devices that stopped reporting.
