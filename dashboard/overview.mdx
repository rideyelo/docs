The dashboard has two summary pages. The **Overview** page shows basic KPIs for one community. The **Global KPIs** page, opened from **KPI** in the sidebar, shows growth and activity across the whole Yelo network.

For definitions of the ride metrics on both pages, see [Ride metrics](/dashboard/ride-metrics).

## Prerequisites

- An admin or intern account to open **Overview**. Interns only see their assigned community.
- An admin account to open **KPI**.

See [Dashboard roles and access](/administration/dashboard-roles).

## Community overview

The **Overview** page is the dashboard home page. Click **Yelo Dashboard** at the top of the sidebar to open it. Fleet managers who open it are redirected to **Fleet**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a community">
    Pick a community from the community selector in the top bar. Interns can't change the selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a time range">
    Use the range menu to pick **Last 7 Days**, **Last 14 Days**, or **Last 30 Days**. The page defaults to **Last 30 Days**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the metrics">
    Each card shows the value for the selected range and the change from the prior period of the same length. **Active Users** and **Active Drivers** are running totals, so their change shows growth over the range.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The page title shows the community name with badges for its state:

- **Active** or **Inactive**, with **(Asleep)** added when the community is asleep.
- The current local time in the community's time zone.

### Overview metrics

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Active Users** | Users in the community with a completed profile, counted as of the end of the range. |
| **Active Drivers** | Users in the community with an active driver application, counted as of the end of the range. |
| **Completion Rate** | Completed confirmed rides divided by confirmed requests. |
| **Ride Requests** | All ride requests, including canceled searches that were never confirmed. |
| **Confirmed Requests** | Rider-confirmed requests, excluding superseded searches. |
| **Completed Rides** | Rides that reached the completed state. |
| **Cancelled Rides** | Rides canceled after a driver was assigned. |
| **Rides Uncomplete** | Confirmed rides that never matched with a driver. |

Below the metrics, the **Community Area** map shows the community's service boundary.

## Global KPIs

Click **KPI** in the sidebar to open **Global KPIs**. The page always covers all markets, so the community selector shows **All markets** and you can't change it. Test users and test communities are excluded from every number.

### Global metrics

Each card shows the all-time total. The change on each card compares what was added in the current calendar week (from Monday 00:00 UTC) with what was added in the previous full week.

| Card | All-time total |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Users** | Users with a completed profile. |
| **Total Drivers** | Users with an active driver application. |
| **GMV** | Sum of fares for completed rides, using the discounted price when one applies. |
| **Attenders** | Distinct users who joined at least one event. |
| **Completed Rides** | Completed rides that weren't canceled. |

<Note>
  Early in the week, the change compares a partial week with a full week, so it often shows a drop.
</Note>

### Weekly trends

Two charts show the last 12 weeks, one point per week. Click a metric above a chart to plot it.

| Chart | Metrics |
| --- | --- |
| **People** | **Users**, **Drivers**, and **Attenders** added each week. |
| **Activity** | **GMV** and **Rides** for each week. |

### Signup acquisition

The **Signup acquisition** card shows where new users found Yelo. It groups sources below 0.5% of signups into **Other / historical**. The center of the chart shows the number of signups in view and the share that has an attributed source.

Use the switches to change which signups are counted. Shares are recalculated when you exclude signups.

| Switch | Effect when off |
| --- | --- |
| **Historical** | Excludes older signups that were created before Yelo captured a source. |
| **No source / unmatched** | Excludes signups with no source or an unmatched source. |

If you see **Attribution backfill is running**, counts can change while older signups are classified.

### Next action queue

The **Next action queue** lists up to seven driver leads that need attention. Leads with pending review items come first, followed by the leads contacted longest ago or never contacted. Only leads in the new, application, pending approval, pending background check, and zero-drives stages appear.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Lead** | Lead name and source. Click it to open the lead on the **Drivers** page. |
| **Community** | The lead's community, or **Unassigned**. |
| **Next action** | The suggested step, such as **Make first contact**, **Follow up**, **Check background status**, or **Help schedule first drive**. |
| **Last contacted** | Date of the last message, or **Never contacted**. |
| **Priority** | **Review ready** when the lead has pending items, otherwise **Follow-up**. |

Click **Open leads** to go to the full pipeline. See [Driver leads](/dashboard/driver-leads).
