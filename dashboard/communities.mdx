A community is one Yelo market, such as a campus and the area around it. Each community has a service area, availability controls, and ride settings. This page covers the two dashboard pages for communities:

- **Communities**, in the **Configuration** group, where admins create and configure every community.
- **Community**, in the **Community** group, which shows metrics, schools, and operating hours for the community selected in the top bar.

For how communities, service areas, and zones relate, see [Communities and zones](/concepts/communities-and-zones). To manage zones and places on a map, see [Community map](/dashboard/community-map).

## Prerequisites

- An `admin` dashboard role to open **Communities** and to edit operating hours.
- An `admin` or `intern` role to open **Community**. Interns only see their assigned community. See [Dashboard roles and access](/administration/dashboard-roles).

## Find a community

Open **Communities** in the **Configuration** group of the sidebar. The page has two tabs:

- **Communities**: community management, described on this page.
- **Schools**: school coordinates and automatic community mapping. See [Schools and communities](/administration/schools-and-communities). The old `/schools` URL opens this tab.

The **Communities** tab starts with the **Community service areas** map. It draws each community's bounding box in one of three colors: **Active**, **Inactive**, or **Selected**. Badges above the map count active and total communities.

To open a community's workspace, do one of these:

- Click its boundary on the map.
- Pick it in the community menu below the map.
- Select its row, or **Manage**, in the **All communities** directory.

The **All communities** directory lists every market, including inactive, hidden, and test communities. Use **Find a community…** to filter by name, primary vehicle type, or timezone.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The community name. |
| **Status** | **Active** or **Inactive**. |
| **Schools** | How many schools belong to the community. |
| **Rides** | **Enabled** or **Disabled**. |
| **Hidden** | Shows **Hidden** when the community is hidden from listings. |
| **Vehicle Type** | The community's primary vehicle type. |
| **Users** | Total users, split into home users and visiting users. |
| **Experimental Features** | Feature keys turned on for the community. |

The selected community is saved in the URL as `?community=`, so you can share a link to its workspace.

## Create a community

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new workspace">
    Select **Add Community**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the community">
    Enter a **Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draw the service area">
    Under **Geography**, select **Draw box**, then click and drag across the community area. Select **Redraw box** to start over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preview">
    Review the automatic **Timezone** and the **Automatic school mapping** preview. See [Timezone](#timezone) and [Preview school changes](/administration/schools-and-communities#preview-school-changes-before-editing-a-community).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Create Community**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

New communities start inactive with rides disabled. Open the new community's workspace to finish its settings and turn it on.

## Configure a community

The community workspace groups every setting for one community. Select **Save Changes** to apply your edits, or **Close workspace** to leave without saving.

### General

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The community name. Required. |
| **Timezone** | Read-only. Yelo sets it from the service area. |

### Controls

| Control | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | Turns the community on. Inactive communities don't count for automatic school mapping. |
| **Hidden** | Hides the community from listings. |
| **Rides Disabled** | Stops Yelo from offering rides in the community. Riders who search for a ride see no Yelo ride options and fall back to third-party options. |
| **Map activity window** | How far back, in hours, anonymized "people heading to…" activity appears on the rider map. Enter a whole number from 1 to 720. The default is 168 hours, which is 7 days. |

### Ride configuration

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary Vehicle Type** | The community's main vehicle type. See [Vehicle types](/dashboard/vehicles). |
| **Primary Fleet** | The community's main fleet, or **None**. Community operating hours can follow this fleet's calendar. See [Operating hours](#operating-hours). |

### Experimental features

Experimental features turn on in-development behavior for one community. Type a feature key, such as `feature_key`, and select **Add** or press Enter. Select the remove icon next to a key to turn it off.

Only add keys that engineering has given you. The dashboard doesn't check whether a key exists.

### Geography

The bounding box is the community's service area. Select **Redraw box** to change it. The dashboard checks the box before saving:

- Latitudes must be between -90 and 90, and longitudes between -180 and 180.
- The north edge must be above the south edge.
- The east edge must be to the right of the west edge.

Changing the bounding box or **Active** reassigns schools when you save. The **Automatic school mapping** panel previews which schools will move. See [Schools and communities](/administration/schools-and-communities).

If another admin changes the same community's service area while you edit, saving fails with "Reload the community before saving its service area." Reload the page and make your change again.

### Timezone

Yelo sets each community's timezone from its service area, including daylight saving time. The **Timezone** panel updates as you draw. If it says the service area needs attention, adjust the bounding box.

## Remove a community

You can remove any community except the default community.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the removal">
    Open the community's workspace and select **Remove**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the impact">
    Read the confirmation, then select **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the name">
    Type the community's exact name and select **Remove community**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Yelo reassigns the community's users and schools to the closest suitable community, or to the default community. Historical records are kept.

## Review community metrics

Open **Community** in the **Community** group of the sidebar. The page shows the community selected in the top bar. Use the time range menu to pick **Last 7 Days**, **Last 14 Days**, or **Last 30 Days**. The default is **Last 30 Days**.

- **Active Users**, **Drivers**, and **Completed rides** show totals for the range, with the change from the previous period.
- **Schools and community** breaks down users by school. Turn off **Include community members (nonstudents)** to see students only.
- **Community schools** lists each school with its **Email domains** and **Students** count. Search by school name or email domain.

The page also previews the rider-facing community feed. See [Preview the community feed](/dashboard/feed-and-events#preview-the-community-feed).

## Operating hours

Admins see an **Operating Hours** card at the bottom of the **Community** page. It controls when the community's market shows as live in the app.

If the community's primary fleet imports its hours from Google Calendar, the card says "Inherited from the community's primary fleet." and shows that calendar. Edit those hours in Google Calendar. See [Fleet operating hours](/admin/operating-hours).

Otherwise, set hours on the card:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on limited hours">
    Turn on **Limited hours**. When it's off, the market runs 24/7 and its status follows whether drivers are online.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set each day">
    Turn on each day the market operates, and set its opening and closing times. New days default to 7:00 PM to 3:00 AM. The day name after the times shows that a window can run past midnight into the next day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The badge at the top of the card shows the market status:

- **LIVE** during an enabled day's window.
- The next scheduled window when the market is off-peak.
- **OFF-PEAK** when no window is scheduled.

If you turn on **Limited hours** with every day off, the market shows **OFF-PEAK** unless drivers are online. Yelo keeps your day settings when you turn **Limited hours** off.
