title: "Send notifications"
description: "Send or schedule push notifications to a community's riders and drivers, and cancel scheduled notifications from the Yelo dashboard."
---

The **Notifications** page lets you send a one-off push notification to a group of people in a community, right away or at a scheduled time. Use it for announcements such as launch news, service changes, or a call for drivers during a busy night.

This page covers sending and scheduling. To edit automated notification copy or review delivery, see [Notification templates](/dashboard/notification-templates). To tune the automatic driver pings Yelo sends for unmatched rides, see [Driver activation](/dashboard/activation). For how notifications work across Yelo, see [Notifications](/concepts/notifications).

## Prerequisites

- An `admin` dashboard role. See [Dashboard roles and access](/administration/dashboard-roles).
- A community selected in the top bar. Every notification goes to one community.

## Open notifications

Open **Notifications** in the **Configuration** group of the sidebar. The page has these tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Templates** | Edit automated notification copy. See [Notification templates](/dashboard/notification-templates). |
| **Analytics** | Compare delivery and tap performance. |
| **History** | Browse every dispatch, including notifications you send from this page. |
| **Scheduled** | Review and cancel notifications queued to send later. |
| **Activation** | Configure driver activation tiers. See [Driver activation](/dashboard/activation). |

Turn on **Show all communities** to view **Analytics** and **History** across every community.

## Send a notification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Select **Send Notification** at the top of the page. The **Community** field shows the community selected in the top bar. You can't change it in the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose recipients">
    Pick a group in **Recipients**. See [Recipient groups](#recipient-groups).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Enter a **Title** of up to 60 characters and a **Body** of up to 200 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally add a link">
    Enter a **Link (optional)**, such as `yelo://ride/123`. It must be a valid URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when to send">
    Under **Delivery**, pick **Send Now**, or pick **Schedule for Later** and enter a **Date** and **Time**. The time uses your computer's timezone and must be in the future.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Select **Send Now** or **Schedule**. Review the community, recipients, title, body, link, and scheduled time in the confirmation dialog. Then select **Send Now** or **Schedule** again, or **Go Back** to make changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A notification you send now goes out immediately. You can't undo it.
</Warning>

Sent notifications appear on the **History** tab. Scheduled notifications appear on the **Scheduled** tab until they send.

### Recipient groups

Yelo only sends to people in the selected community who have a push token and allow notifications.

| Recipients | Who receives it |
| --- | --- |
| **All Users** | Everyone in the community. |
| **Drivers Only** | Active drivers. |
| **Riders Only** | Everyone who isn't an active driver. |
| **Online Drivers** | Active drivers who are online now. |
| **Recently Online Drivers** | Active drivers whose last driving session ended within the past 60 minutes. |

The confirmation dialog and the **Scheduled** table label **Drivers Only** as **All Drivers**, and **Riders Only** as **All Riders**.

## Review scheduled notifications

The **Scheduled** tab lists queued notifications for the community selected in the top bar. The tab label shows how many are queued.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | The notification title. |
| **Body** | The message. Hover to see the full text. |
| **Recipients** | The recipient group. |
| **Sender** | The admin who scheduled it. |
| **Sends At** | The scheduled time, plus how long until it sends. |
| **Actions** | **Cancel**, when the notification can still be cancelled. |

Sort by **Title** or **Sends At**, and use **Rows per page** to page through long lists.

## Cancel a scheduled notification

1. On the **Scheduled** tab, select **Cancel** on the notification's row.
2. Check the title and scheduled time in the **Cancel Scheduled Notification** dialog.
3. Select **Yes, Cancel**. Select **No, Keep It** to leave it scheduled.

You can't undo a cancellation. To send the message later, schedule it again.
