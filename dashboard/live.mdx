title: "Live operations"
description: "Watch ride-enabled markets in real time, track drivers and rides on a live map, review service quality, and set surge pricing from the Live page in the Yelo dashboard"
---

The **Live** page is the real-time operations view for Yelo's markets. Use it to see which markets have rides in progress, where drivers and riders are, how quickly riders get matched and picked up, and how Yelo's fares compare with other ride apps. You can also set temporary surge pricing from this page.

The page covers one market at a time. Pick the market from the cards at the top.

## Prerequisites

- An admin account. See [Dashboard roles and access](/administration/dashboard-roles).

## Open Live

Click **Live** in the **Global** group of the sidebar. Admins also land on **Live** when they open the dashboard home page or click **Yelo Dashboard** at the top of the sidebar.

The page refreshes itself while the browser tab is visible. Click **Refreshes every 10s** in the header to reload everything immediately. If a refresh fails, the page shows **Some live data could not be refreshed. Last available data may be stale.** and keeps the last data it loaded. Click **Retry** to try again.

| Section | Refresh interval |
| --- | --- |
| Market cards, **Drivers** table, live rides, and map markers | 10 seconds |
| **Rides** table history filters | 30 seconds |
| **Live performance** metrics, **Service quality**, **Ride journey**, and configured pricing | 1 minute |
| Observed competitor quotes | 5 minutes |

## Select a market

Each card at the top of the page is one market. Only active communities with rides turned on appear. Click a card to open that market. The dashboard remembers the selection and uses it as the community selected in the sidebar.

| Card item | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Status dot | Green when the market is inside its operating hours (**LIVE**). Gray when it's off peak. See [Operating hours](/admin/operating-hours). |
| **live rides** | Rides that are confirmed, queued, driver en route, driver waiting, or in progress. |
| **drivers online** | Drivers with the Drive screen open in the last two minutes, within 10 km of the market. Shows **—** when live presence is unavailable. |
| **riders online** | Other app users with the app open in the last two minutes. |

<Note>
  Online counts come from live app presence, not from open driving sessions. A driver who is signed in to drive but hasn't reported in the last two minutes doesn't count as online.
</Note>

## Read live performance

The **Performance** header shows the selected market. Pick a period of **24 hours**, **7 days**, **30 days**, or **All time**. The default is **24 hours**. The period applies to the time riders requested their rides. The period resets to **24 hours** when you reload the page.

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **New users** | Users who completed their profile in the period. |
| **New drivers** | Drivers whose application was activated in the period. |
| **Completed rides** | Rides requested in the period that completed without being canceled, including rides waiting for the rider's rating. |
| **Incomplete rides** | Rides requested in the period that found no driver, were canceled before a driver was assigned, or have been stuck for more than an hour. |
| **Request completion** | **Completed rides** divided by every ride request in the period, as a percentage. The change is shown in percentage points (**pp**). |

Each card compares the period with the one right before it, such as the previous 7 days. **All time** has no comparison. When the previous period is zero, the card shows the change without a percentage.

<Warning>
  **Request completion** counts every request, including searches the rider never confirmed. It's lower than the **Completion Rate** on **Rides** > **KPIs**, which only counts confirmed requests. **Incomplete rides** also uses a broader definition than **Uncompleted Rides**. See [Ride metrics](/dashboard/ride-metrics).
</Warning>

## Use the live map

The map shows the selected market's current activity. It updates in place every 10 seconds and fits itself to the visible content when that content moves. Once you pan, zoom, or rotate the map yourself, it keeps your view on later refreshes. Switching markets or clicking the locate button resets the view.

- Drivers appear as photo markers. A dashed photo border means the position is the driver's last known location rather than a live one.
- Offline drivers appear only if they were last online within the selected **Performance** period. Under **All time**, the map only shows offline drivers who were online within the last 30 days. The legend in the layer panel explains which limit applies.
- Riders with an active ride stay on the map even after their app stops reporting presence.
- Ride routes are the stored quoted routes for live rides, not GPS traces.
- Locations more than 10 km outside the market are left off the map.

Hover over a driver or ride in the tables below the map to highlight it on the map.

Click a driver photo, or hover over a user marker, to see who it is, whether the position is a live presence location or a last known location, and when Yelo observed it. Markers without a recorded time show **Location timestamp unavailable**.

Open the layer panel in the top-left corner of the map to turn layers on or off:

| Layer | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Drivers** | Drivers online now, plus last-known locations of offline drivers who were online within the selected period, capped at 30 days. |
| **Online users / active riders** | Users with the app open, and riders on an active ride. |
| **Other users** | Last-known locations of other users in the market. These only load while **Last known users** is on. |
| **Requests** | Routes for requests that don't have a confirmed ride yet. A request stays on the map for five minutes. |
| **Ride routes** | Routes for live rides. |
| **Last known users** | Loads last-known locations for other users in the market. Off by default. |
| **Zones** | The market's pricing zones. On by default. |

Click the locate button to fit the map to the live content again. Click the expand button to open the map full screen, and click it again to exit.

## Review service quality

The **Service quality** card covers the selected period. The badge shows **LIVE** or **Off peak** for the market right now.

| Metric | Measured from |
| --- | --- |
| **TTM** | Confirmation to match. |
| **TTP** | Match to pickup. |
| **Wait** | Confirmation to pickup, which is **TTM** plus **TTP**. |

Each metric shows the average, with the minimum and maximum below it.

Below the timings, the card counts ride problems in the period: **Cancelled by rider**, **Cancelled by driver**, **Unmatched**, and **Failed after pickup**. Click any count, or click **Review ride issues**, to open **Rides** > **Issues** for this market and period. See [Rides](/dashboard/rides#issues-tab).

The **Operating hours** panel shows the market's weekly schedule in the market's time zone, with today highlighted. Markets without limited hours show **24 hours** for every day. When the market is off peak, the panel shows when it next goes live.

## Monitor drivers

The **Drivers** table lists the market's activated drivers who are online now, or who drove or were seen in the last 7 days. The table also follows the **Performance** period. Offline drivers only appear if they were last active within that period. Drivers whose location is more than 10 km outside the market are left out. Click **All drivers** to open the market's full driver directory. See [Drivers](/dashboard/drivers).

Filter the table with **Online**, **Offline**, or **All**. The table opens on **Online**.

| Filter | Drivers shown |
| --- | --- |
| **Online** | Drivers who are **Driving** or **Idle**, plus drivers who went offline within the last 4 hours. |
| **Offline** | Drivers with the **Offline** status. |
| **All** | Every driver in the table. |

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Driving** | The driver has a queued or active ride. |
| **Idle** | The driver is online with no ride. |
| **Offline** | The driver isn't online. |

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Driver** | Name, user tags, and fleet. Click the name to open the user's full details. |
| **Status** | One of the statuses above. |
| **Recent activity** | The driver's latest decline today, or when the driver was last seen, with the number of rides completed today in the market's time zone. |

### Open a driver's operations sheet

Click the **Recent activity** cell to open a compact sheet with the driver's operations context. The header shows the driver's photo, phone number, tags, and status. Click **Full profile** to open the full user detail sheet.

The sheet contains:

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Session stats | **Session duration** for online drivers, or **Last session** for offline drivers. Also shows **Last driver activity** and **Completed today**. |
| **Active ride & queue** | The driver's active ride and dispatch queue, in order. Each queued ride shows the rider, pickup, destination, and whether it was **Queued** or **Auto queued**. Click a ride to open its details. |
| **Declines & cancellations** | Today's declines with their reasons, and recent canceled drives with who canceled. The badge shows today's declines and all-time cancellations. |
| **Driver history · all time** | **Drives**, **Cancellations**, and **Rating**, with the join date, fleet, and vehicle type. |
| **Earnings** | **Awaiting rating**, **Stripe available**, and **Stripe pending**. |
| **Fleet, vehicle & application** | The driver's fleet memberships and driver application, with a link to the fleet and vehicles page. |
| **Quo · messages & contact** | Recent Quo messages with the driver. See [Quo messages](/dashboard/user-directory#quo-messages). |
| **Device & telemetry** | Phone, app version, device, platform, OS, OTA update, and last seen time, with a link to [Telemetry](/dashboard/telemetry). |
| **Drive history** | Recent drives with the status or who canceled, and the expected payout. |
| **Notification log** | Notifications sent to the driver, with delivery status and errors. Click **Load more** to see up to 100. |

**Awaiting rating** is the expected payout, in dollars, for the driver's completed rides that are still waiting for the rider's rating. It's separate from the Stripe balances. Payment capture can wait up to 24 hours for tips.

The sheet refreshes dispatch, earnings, and notifications every 15 seconds, and the driver's profile every 30 seconds. If a refresh fails, the sheet keeps the last data it loaded.

## Monitor rides

The **Rides** table shows the market's live rides by default. Pick a different view from the menu:

| View | Rides shown |
| --- | --- |
| **Live / active** | Confirmed rides that aren't finished or canceled, plus unconfirmed requests from the last five minutes. |
| **Completed · 24h** | Completed rides requested in the last 24 hours. |
| **Cancelled · 24h** | Canceled rides requested in the last 24 hours. |
| **All rides · 24h** | Every ride requested in the last 24 hours. |

The 24-hour views show 50 rides per page. Use **Previous** and **Next** to page through them. Click **All rides** to open **Rides** > **Trips** for this market.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Rider** | The rider, with the assigned driver below, or **Awaiting driver**. Click the rider's name to open the user's details. |
| **Status** | The ride's current status. |
| **Requested** | Time since the request. |
| **TTM**, **TTP**, **TTD** | Time to match, time to pickup, and time to dropoff. |
| **Time** | Rider time for the ride. |
| **Distance** | Ride distance. |
| **Fare** | The ride price, after any discount. |

Click a row to open **Ride Details** with the route map and ride timeline. See [Rides](/dashboard/rides#open-ride-details).

## Check pricing

The **Pricing** card summarizes what riders pay in the selected market. Click **Manage** to open the market on the **Pricing Management** page. See [Ride pricing](/dashboard/ride-pricing).

- **Yelo average completed fare** is the average fare of completed rides in the selected period.
- The configs table lists each pricing config's **Fleet**, **Vehicle**, **Avg 3 mi** (the quoted price for a 3-mile, 9-minute ride), and **Featured** (**Always shown** or **Off**). Click a row to edit that config.
- **Observed standard-car quotes** shows the average **UberX** and **Lyft Standard** prices that the Yelo app saw in this market during the period, with the number of observations and when the last one was seen.

<Note>
  Competitor quotes come from what riders' apps loaded, across many different routes. They aren't matched-route comparisons or live offers. If the page shows **Quote analytics connection is not configured.**, the API has no PostHog query credentials.
</Note>

## Set or clear surge pricing

Surge multiplies the configured fare for new quotes in the market until it expires. See [Pricing](/concepts/pricing).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Move the Surge slider">
    At the bottom of the **Pricing** card, drag **Surge** to a value from **1.0x** to **3.0x**, in steps of 0.1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Apply">
    **Apply** appears once the value differs from the current multiplier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a duration and confirm">
    In the **Set Surge** dialog, choose a **Duration** of **1 hour** to **4 hours**. Click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While surge is active, the card shows the multiplier and the time left, such as **1.5x surge - 42m left**. Surge ends on its own when the duration runs out.

To end surge early, drag the slider back to **1.0x**, click **Apply**, and confirm in the **Clear Surge** dialog.

<Warning>
  Surge changes rider prices immediately. Yelo records who set each surge, but the dashboard doesn't ask for a reason.
</Warning>

## Follow the ride journey

The **Ride journey** chart shows how every ride requested in the period moved through its stages: requested, confirmed, matched, queued or en route, at pickup, and in progress. Band widths show ride counts. Rides that left the flow branch off below the stage where they stopped:

- **Completed**
- **Rider canceled**, **Driver canceled**, or **System canceled**
- **Pending / unmatched**, for requests that didn't advance or found no driver

Pick a starting point of **Requested**, **Confirmed**, **Matched**, or **In progress**. The default is **Confirmed**. Scroll the chart horizontally to see later stages.

<Note>
  The chart counts every ride in the period. It doesn't depend on the rides loaded in the **Rides** table.
</Note>

## Contact drivers and riders

The **Live** page doesn't have controls to notify a whole market or ping one driver or rider. Admins can still send these push notifications through the dashboard API. See [Dashboard analytics internals](/engineering/domains/dashboard-analytics#notify-and-ping).

To reach a driver another way, open the driver's details from the **Drivers** table and use the phone number or Quo panel. See [User directory](/dashboard/user-directory#quo-messages).
