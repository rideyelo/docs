---
title: "QR payments"
description: "Create QR payment links for a fleet, set fixed or custom pricing, deactivate links, and review QR payment history in the Yelo dashboard."
---

QR payment links let riders pay a fleet directly, for example by scanning a QR code in a vehicle. Each link is a QR config: a short URL tied to one fleet, with either a fixed price or a price range the rider chooses from. You manage QR configs and see payment history on the **Payments** tab of the **Fleet** page.

The old `/qr-payments` URL redirects to the **Fleet** page.

## Prerequisites

- An `admin` or `fleet` dashboard role. Fleet managers see and edit only QR configs and payments for their assigned fleets. See [Dashboard roles and access](/administration/dashboard-roles).
- An `admin` role to create a QR config.
- A fleet with a connected Stripe account. Yelo creates the payment objects on the fleet's Stripe account, and rejects the config if the fleet has none. See [Fleets](/dashboard/fleets#operations).

## Open QR payments

1. Open **Fleet** in the **Community** group of the sidebar.
2. Select the **Payments** tab.

The **QR Payments** section shows a card for each QR config, followed by **QR Payment History**.

Each card shows:

- The short link, such as `pay.yelo.link/campus-carts`, as the card title.
- The fleet name.
- The pricing, such as `Fixed: $5.00` or `Custom: $3.00 - $20.00 (preset $5.00)`.
- An **Active** or **Inactive** badge.

## Create a QR config

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Select **Create New** on the **Add a new QR config** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the fleet">
    Pick the **Fleet**. The list shows each fleet with its community.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the slug">
    Enter a **Slug**. It becomes the link path, `pay.yelo.link/{slug}`. Use lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens, and start with a letter or number. The form strips other characters as you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the product">
    Enter a **Product Name**. Riders see it at checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose pricing">
    Pick a **Pricing Mode** and enter the amounts in dollars. See [Pricing modes](#pricing-modes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Create Config**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Yelo creates a Stripe product, price, and payment link on the fleet's Stripe account. It then creates the `pay.yelo.link` short link, which points to the rider tip page for that slug. If the short link can't be created, Yelo still saves the config without it.

### Pricing modes

| Mode | Fields | Rider experience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fixed** | **Amount (dollars)** | The rider pays the set amount. |
| **Custom** | **Minimum (dollars)**, **Maximum (dollars)**, **Preset Amount (dollars)** | The rider picks an amount between the minimum and maximum. The preset is the suggested amount. |

## Change pricing

1. Select **Edit Pricing** on the config's card.
2. Choose **Fixed** or **Custom** and enter the new amounts.
3. Select **Save Pricing**.

Changing pricing creates a new Stripe price and payment link, and then deactivates the old payment link. The short link keeps the same slug, so QR codes you've already printed keep working.

## Deactivate a QR config

Select **Deactivate** on an active config's card. Yelo deactivates the Stripe payment link, and the card shows **Inactive**. Riders can no longer pay through that link.

## Review payment history

**QR Payment History** lists payments made through your QR configs.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer Name** | The payer's name. |
| **Email** | The payer's email. |
| **Phone** | The payer's phone number. |
| **Amount** | The amount paid. |
| **Fleet** | The fleet that received the payment. Admins only. |
| **Date / Time** | When the payment happened. Hover to see the exact time. |

To narrow the list:

- Search by name or email with **Search name or email...**.
- Pick a start and end date with the date fields.

Use **Rows per page** and the page controls to move through long histories. The **QR Payments** KPI on the **Overview** tab summarizes the same payments for the selected time range.

For how QR payments fit into fleet payouts, see [Payments and payouts](/concepts/payments-and-payouts) and [Events and the community feed](/concepts/events-and-feed).
