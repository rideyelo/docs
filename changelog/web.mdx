This page lists merged pull requests in [`rideyelo/yelo-web`](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web) since August 25, 2026, newest first. Each week is split into:

- **Dashboard**: the admin dashboard at `dash.rideyelo.com`.
- **Public sites and sign-in**: `joinyelo.com` marketing pages, the AuthV3 sign-in and signup flow, invite and referral links, and app links.
- **Tooling**: CI, deployment, dependencies, and the E2E run viewer.

Most dashboard changes depend on a paired `yelo-api` pull request, which ships first. Automated OpenAPI contract syncs from the bot are listed together under **Tooling**.

<Update label="Week of September 21, 2026" description="Live operations replaces Supply & Demand, silent ride cancellation, and fleet membership editing" tags={["Dashboard", "Public sites"]}>
  **Dashboard**

  - The new **Live** page replaces **Supply & Demand**. It shows ride-enabled market cards, performance metrics, a live map of drivers, riders, and ride routes, compact driver and ride tables, service quality, operating hours, pricing and competitor quotes, and a ride journey chart. Data refreshes every 10 seconds. `/presence` and `/driver-ops` now open **Live**, and `/supply-demand` no longer exists. The account menu and community selector moved into the sidebar, and the dashboard home page now sends admins to **Live**, interns to **Community**, and fleet managers to **Fleets**. See [Live operations](/dashboard/live). ([#158](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/158))
  - Admins can silently cancel a free, accepted ride from **Ride Details**, below the ride timeline. The dashboard checks eligibility first, requires a reason, and sends no notifications. See [Cancel a ride silently](/dashboard/rides#cancel-a-ride-silently) and [Cancellations](/concepts/cancellations#admin-silent-cancellation). ([#159](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/159))
  - Admins can add and remove a driver's fleet memberships and pick the active fleet from the user detail sheet, with a review step before saving. Fleet deletion is now worded as archival, and the confirmation stays open when dependency checks fail. See [Manage fleet memberships](/dashboard/user-directory#manage-fleet-memberships) and [Fleets](/dashboard/fleets). ([#156](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/156))
  - Community top locations now use four explicit slots that point to Yelo places, with visibility, manual pins, and 24-hour automatic refresh controls. See [Community top locations](/guides/community-top-locations). ([#152](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/152))
  - The default community appears in market pickers even when it's inactive, so you can reach its searches and top-location settings. ([#154](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/154))
  - The lead Quo panel says when the phone on file isn't valid, instead of showing a generic error. See [Driver leads](/dashboard/driver-leads#quo-messages). ([#153](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/153))
  - Community overview, ride KPIs, and fleet overview show confirmed requests next to ride requests. Completion rate is explained as completed confirmed rides divided by confirmed requests. See [Ride metrics](/dashboard/ride-metrics). ([#151](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/151))

  **Public sites and sign-in**

  - The `/drivers` hero now has a **text us** pill that opens a pre-filled text to the driver number, replacing the phone-number capture form. Route badges are centered on phones. See [Public sites](/engineering/web/public-sites). ([#138](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/138))

  **Tooling**

  - Dependency updates, including Orval 8.34.0 and Mapbox 3.31.0. ([#150](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/150))
</Update>

<Update label="Week of September 14, 2026" description="Global search, navigation consolidation, fleet calendars, marketing segments, and fleet vehicle inventory" tags={["Dashboard", "Public sites"]}>
  **Dashboard**

  - Global search finds pages, communities, fleets, and users, and jumps straight to them. Community editing moved below the map, and **Rides** became global-first with market filters in the URL. Issues and recent searches moved to sortable tables. The **GTM** pages were removed. See [Rides](/dashboard/rides) and [User directory](/dashboard/user-directory#global-search). ([#131](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/131))
  - Navigation was consolidated. **Activation** moved under **Notifications**, **Schools** under **Communities**, and driver operations under **Drivers**. **Access** and **Jobs** moved into a collapsed **Administration** group. Old links redirect. Interns and fleet managers only see controls their role can use. See [Dashboard roles and access](/administration/dashboard-roles). ([#139](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/139))
  - Fleet, community, and user management were unified. Calendar IDs are editable, community feed and hours moved into **Community**, hourly volume heatmaps were added, and invitations moved into the driver directory. Users gained automatic fleet tags, admin profile editing, and search by name, phone, or UUID. See [Communities](/dashboard/communities) and [User directory](/dashboard/user-directory). ([#136](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/136))
  - Fleet editors became full pages with **Settings**, **Operations**, and **Vehicles**. Vehicle types and a searchable vehicle inventory moved into fleets, with admin create, edit, and delete for shared and personal vehicles. `/vehicles` redirects to fleets. Vehicle artwork now comes from the mobile release catalog. See [Fleet vehicles](/dashboard/fleet-vehicles). ([#145](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/145))
  - Imported fleet and community schedules from Google Calendar are shown with zone filters, time zone, and sync status. A seven-day calendar view, a zone map, and admin prefix editing followed. Imported hours don't affect ride visibility. See [Service communities and calendars](/engineering/fleets/service-communities-and-calendars). ([#124](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/124), [#133](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/133))
  - Admins can activate a driver awaiting background clearance from the lead sheet, after confirming manual clearance. See [Activate a driver](/dashboard/driver-leads#activate-a-driver). ([#134](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/134))
  - The notification editor puts copy variants first, adds variable buttons and inline validation, and replaces UUID filters with searchable pickers. Logs and history are merged into **History**, with daily dispatch charts. See [Notifications](/dashboard/notifications) and [Notification templates](/dashboard/notification-templates). ([#135](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/135))
  - **Contact sync** under **Administration** shows Quo and Resend sync progress, refreshing every 15 seconds. Admins can build and monitor marketing segments with all/any rules and audience previews. See [Contact sync](/administration/contact-sync). ([#140](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/140), [#144](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/144))
  - Pricing uses compact pricing-type and fare-adjustment menus. Jitter values moved into a platform-admin global settings dialog. See [Global jitter settings](/dashboard/ride-pricing#global-jitter-settings). ([#141](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/141))
  - Schools with no students are collapsed by default. Telemetry gained an include-not-determined toggle for permissions and time filters for app versions. See [Telemetry](/dashboard/telemetry). ([#137](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/137))
  - Fleet invitations describe owner-operator vehicle and Stripe requirements, on both the dashboard and the public invite page. See [Driver invitations](/fleets/driver-invitations). ([#148](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/148))

  **Public sites and sign-in**

  - AuthV3 keeps an in-progress app sign-in across page reloads, and no longer starts an unrelated web signup when app context is missing. See [Signup and auth site](/engineering/web/signup-and-auth-site). ([#142](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/142))
  - The invite site root and `/index.html` redirect to `signup.yelo.link`. ([#130](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/130))
  - The contact sheet's **become a yelo partner** option now opens the `/partner` venues page. ([#127](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/127))

  **Tooling**

  - The E2E run viewer shows ride workflow coverage, including untested cases. ([#149](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/149))
  - 24 dependency updates, including React 19.3, Vite 8.3, and Mermaid 12. ([#132](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/132))
</Update>

Changing a community's bounds or status previews which schools move. See [Schools and communities](/administration/schools-and-communities). ([#112](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/112))
  - The user detail sheet shows contact matching and friend diagnostics. See [Registered contacts](/dashboard/user-directory#registered-contacts). ([#123](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/123))
  - The pricing editor accepts an optional rider-facing title and image filename for each ride option. See [Rider-facing ride options](/dashboard/ride-pricing#rider-facing-ride-options). ([#122](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/122))
  - The **On map** locations tab pages through every location instead of stopping at 200. ([#119](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/119))
  - Driver tag suggestions, such as **Founding Driver**, appear before you type. ([#120](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/120))

  **Public sites and sign-in**

  - New `/vision` page on `joinyelo.com`. The desktop nav drops the rider/driver switch, and every site uses the app icon as its favicon. Review follow-ups added a shared contact sheet, full footer links on compact pages, and site-wide careers links. See [Public sites](/engineering/web/public-sites). ([#107](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/107), [#115](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/115))
  - AuthV3 resumes a signup after a page remount or a lost phone-verification response, and uses the server's exact callback URL. See [Signup and auth site](/engineering/web/signup-and-auth-site). ([#118](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/118))
  - iOS referral links fall back to the App Store with the referral code, and `invite.yelo.link` publishes an app association file that allows universal links. Android app links trust the Google Play signing certificate. See [Deep links and URLs](/reference/deep-links-and-urls). ([#117](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/117), [#121](https://github.com/rideyelo/yelo-web/pull/121))
