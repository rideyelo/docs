---
title: "Fleet driver invitations"
description: "Invite drivers to your fleet with a reusable link, and learn how fleet-managed payouts differ from owner-operator enrollment."
---

A fleet driver invitation is a single reusable link that adds drivers to your fleet. Drivers open the link, sign in or create a Yelo account in the mobile app, and join. Your fleet becomes their active fleet, and they join your fleet's community.

Use invitation links when you want to onboard drivers yourself instead of asking Yelo to assign them.

## Fleet-managed payouts and owner-operator enrollment

What a driver must complete after joining depends on your fleet's payment model.

| | Fleet-managed payouts | Owner-operator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who receives driver payouts | Your fleet's Stripe account | The driver's personal Stripe account |
| Vehicle | Registered through the driver application | The driver's own vehicle |
| Personal Stripe setup | Not required. The Stripe step shows **Payouts managed by** your fleet. | Required |
| Requirement to create an invitation link | Fleet is active and its Stripe payout setup is complete | Fleet is active and custom payouts are disabled |
| Invitation wording | "Drive with *fleet* and have payouts managed by the fleet." | "Drive with *fleet* using your own vehicle and Stripe account." |

<Note>
  Joining a fleet never skips application steps. Every driver still completes the normal profile photo, license, vehicle, and phone checks. Owner operators also complete personal Stripe onboarding.
</Note>

When a driver joins your fleet, Yelo:

- Makes your fleet the driver's active fleet and removes the driver from any previous fleet.
- Switches the driver to your fleet's community.
- Clears any previous fleet-managed payout association.
- Activates the driver automatically once all remaining application steps are complete.

## Manage your invitation link

### Prerequisites

- Access to the fleet in the Yelo dashboard.
- An active fleet that can accept drivers:
  - **Fleet-managed payouts**: the fleet's Stripe payout setup is complete.
  - **Owner-operator**: custom payouts are disabled for the fleet.

If your fleet doesn't meet these requirements, the invitation card shows an **Invitations unavailable** badge and you can't create or rotate a link.

### Open the invitation card

Open the **Driver invitation** card in either place:

- On the **Fleets** page, click **Invite drivers** next to the **Fleet Driver Directory**.
- In a fleet's detail panel, open the **Invite drivers** tab.

The card shows a **Ready** badge when your fleet can accept drivers.

### Create a link

Click **Create driver join link**. Yelo generates a link in this format:

```text
https://yelo.link/fleet-<code>
```

The link is reusable. Share it with as many drivers as you like.

### Copy a link

Click **Copy** next to the link to copy it to your clipboard. Share it by text, email, or any other channel.

### Rotate a link

Rotate the link if it was shared somewhere it shouldn't have been, or if you want to stop new drivers from using an old link.

1. Click **Rotate link**.
2. Confirm by clicking **Rotate link** in the dialog.

The current link stops accepting drivers immediately. Share the new link with drivers who haven't joined yet.

### Disable a link

1. Click **Disable link**.
2. Confirm by clicking **Disable link** in the dialog.

Drivers who already joined stay in your fleet. The link no longer accepts new drivers. To invite drivers again, click **Create driver join link** to generate a new link.

## Join a fleet as a driver

### Prerequisites

- An invitation link from the fleet manager.
- The Yelo app on your phone. If you don't have it, download it from the App Store through the invitation page.

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation link">
    Tap the link the fleet manager shared. If the Yelo app is installed, the link opens it directly. Otherwise, the invitation page opens in your browser and shows the fleet name, logo, and how payouts work for this fleet.

    Tap **Open Yelo to join** to continue in the app, or download the app from the App Store first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the invitation">
    The app shows the fleet name, the fleet community, and how you get paid:

    - **Fleet-managed payouts**: "The fleet manages your driver payouts."
    - **Owner-operator**: "You'll use your own vehicle and Stripe account. Complete all required driver application steps before driving."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or create an account">
    If you aren't signed in, tap **Sign in or create account**. The app remembers the invitation and brings you back to it after you sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the fleet">
    Tap **Join** followed by the fleet name. The fleet becomes your active fleet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish driver setup">
    Tap **Continue**. If application steps remain, the app opens driver setup. Complete the remaining items and Yelo activates you automatically.

    - With fleet-managed payouts, the Stripe step reads **Payouts managed by** your fleet. You don't need to connect a personal Stripe account.
    - As an owner operator, you connect your own Stripe account to receive payouts.

    If no steps remain, the app opens the driver hub.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you're already a member of the fleet, the app skips the invitation and returns you to the home screen.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="This fleet link isn't available">
    The link was rotated, disabled, or is invalid. Ask the fleet manager for their current driver invitation link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The fleet can't accept drivers right now">
    The fleet is inactive or doesn't meet the requirements for its payment model. For fleet-managed payouts, the fleet's Stripe payout setup must be complete. For owner-operator fleets, custom payouts must be disabled. Contact the fleet manager and try again later.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Create driver join link button is disabled">
    Your fleet shows **Invitations unavailable**. Check that the fleet is active. For fleet-managed payouts, complete the fleet's Stripe payout setup. For owner-operator fleets, each driver completes their own Stripe setup, so check the fleet's settings instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## API reference

The Yelo API exposes the invitation flow through these endpoints.

| Method | Path | Auth | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GET` | `/fleet-join/{code}` | Public | Preview a join link |
| `POST` | `/driver/fleet-join/{code}` | Driver | Join the fleet |

The preview response includes `payouts_managed_by_fleet`. It is `true` for fleet-managed payouts and `false` for owner-operator fleets. It also includes `available`, which is `false` when the fleet can't accept drivers.

The join response lists outstanding application steps in `remaining_steps`. Possible values are `profile_picture`, `license`, `vehicle`, and `stripe`. The `stripe` step appears only for owner operators who haven't finished personal Stripe onboarding.

```json
{
  "fleet_id": "3f6c2a9e-8b1d-4c7a-9e2f-1a5b6c7d8e9f",
  "fleet_name": "Downtown Drivers",
  "community_id": "b2d4f6a8-1c3e-4a5b-8d7f-9e0a1b2c3d4e",
  "community_changed": true,
  "driver_application_active": false,
  "result": "joined",
  "remaining_steps": ["vehicle", "stripe"]
}
```

`result` is `joined` for a new member or `already_joined` if the driver was already in the fleet. If the fleet can't accept drivers, the API returns an error with the message "This fleet cannot accept drivers right now."
