This guide is for fleet managers. It walks through everything your account can open in the Yelo dashboard: the **Fleet** page, the **Drivers** page, and the **Rides** page.

Your account has the `fleet` role and one or more fleet assignments. Each assignment pairs a fleet with a community. Everything you see is limited to those fleets. For the full role rules, see [Fleet manager](/administration/dashboard-roles#fleet-manager).

## Prerequisites

- A dashboard invitation from a Yelo admin, with at least one fleet assigned. See [Invite a user](/dashboard/access-and-settings#invite-a-user).

## Find your way around

When you sign in, the dashboard opens the **Fleet** page. The sidebar header reads **Fleet Portal**.

- **The fleet card.** The card under the sidebar header shows the fleet you're viewing and its community. You can't change it from the sidebar.
- **Three pages.** You can open **Fleet**, **Drivers**, and **Rides**. Every other sidebar item is dimmed. If you open the URL of another page, the dashboard sends you back to **Fleet**.
- **Search.** Click **Search…** in the top bar, or press <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on Windows), to jump to a page or a community.

See [Navigate the dashboard](/dashboard/navigation) for the sidebar, search, **Recent views**, and theme buttons.

### Switch fleets and communities

Skip this section if you manage one fleet.

| To switch | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Fleets in the same community | On **Fleet**, use the fleet dropdown next to the time range menu. On **Drivers**, use the **Fleet** selector. Both appear as usable controls only when you have more than one fleet in the selected community. |
| Communities | Open search, type the community name, and select it under **Communities**. The dashboard opens **Fleet** for that community. |

The dashboard starts on the community of your first fleet assignment. It remembers the community you last selected in your browser.

The fleet you pick on **Fleet** and the fleet you pick on **Drivers** are remembered separately. **Rides** has no fleet selector.

## Fleet

The **Fleet** page shows how one fleet is performing. The page title is your fleet's name, and the line under it reads "Operating in" followed by the community.

The time range menu at the top right starts on **Last 7 Days**. It applies to the KPIs and the chart on the **Overview** tab.

The page has three tabs.

### Overview

The **Overview** tab is where you check the fleet's numbers.

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| KPI cards | **Ride Requests**, **Confirmed Requests**, **Completed Rides**, **Cancelled Rides**, **Rides Uncomplete**, **Completion Rate**, **Total Earnings**, **Market Users**, and **QR Payments**. **QR Payments** shows the number of payments with the amount in parentheses. |
| **Ride Requests Over Time** | A chart of ride requests for the selected time range, in the community's timezone. |
| Driver cards | **Total Drivers**, **Approved Drivers**, **Avg. Rating**, and **Total Drives** for the drivers in the directory below. |
| **Fleet Driver Directory** | Your fleet's drivers, sorted by **Total Earnings (GMV)**. Click a column heading to sort by it. Rows don't open anything for fleet managers. |
| **Invite drivers** | The button next to **Fleet Driver Directory**. It opens your fleet's driver invitation link. |
| **Fleet Pricing Management** | The active pricing configs for your fleet in this community. |

For what each KPI counts, see [Ride metrics](/dashboard/ride-metrics). For every item on the tab, see [Monitor a fleet](/dashboard/fleets#monitor-a-fleet).

To add drivers, share your invitation link. See [Fleet driver invitations](/fleets/driver-invitations).

### Payments

The **Payments** tab opens on **QR Payments**. It has two parts:

- A card for each QR payment link, with **Edit Pricing** and **Deactivate**.
- **QR Payment History**, which lists each payment with **Customer Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Amount**, and **Date / Time**.

The tab also shows a **Create New** button. Creating a link needs a Yelo admin. See [What needs a Yelo admin?](/support/fleet-manager-faq#what-needs-a-yelo-admin).

For how the links work, see [QR payments](/dashboard/qr-payments).

### Operating hours

The **Operating hours** tab shows **Imported operating hours**: the schedule Yelo imports from your fleet's Google Calendar, with the community timezone and the **Last imported** time.

You don't edit hours in the dashboard. Click **Edit in Google Calendar** to open the calendar in Google. The calendar owner must share editing access with your Google account. Yelo checks Google about every five minutes.

If the tab says "No operating-hours calendar is attached to this fleet and community.", ask a Yelo admin to attach one. See [Fleet operating hours](/admin/operating-hours) and [How do I set my fleet's hours?](/support/fleet-manager-faq#how-do-i-set-my-fleets-hours).

## Drivers

The **Drivers** page lists the drivers in your fleet. For fleet managers it has one tab, **Drivers**. The lead pipeline tabs that admins and interns see aren't shown.

The heading shows the community name followed by **Drivers**, with a badge that says whether the community is **Active** or **Inactive**.

If you have more than one fleet in the selected community, a **Fleet** selector appears under the heading. Pick a fleet to change the roster.

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Metric cards | **Total Drivers**, **Approved Drivers**, **In Process**, **Ready for Review**, **Avg. Rating**, and **Total Drives**. |
| **Active Drivers** > **Driver Directory** | One row per driver, with **Photo**, **Name**, **Drives**, **Cancels (Driver+Rider)**, **Rating**, **Last Active**, and **Status**. Click a column heading to sort. The list starts sorted by **Drives**. |

**Status** is **Approved** or **Pending**. **Rating** shows **Unrated** until a driver has a rating. **Last Active** shows **Never** for a driver who hasn't been active.

<Note>
  **In Process** and **Ready for Review** always show 0 for fleet managers. The dashboard only loads application queues for admins. The **In Process** and **Ready for Review** tabs are hidden for the same reason.
</Note>

Rows don't open anything for fleet managers. Only admins can review a driver's application. See [What needs a Yelo admin?](/support/fleet-manager-faq#what-needs-a-yelo-admin).

## Rides

The **Rides** page shows "Trips for your assigned fleets." For fleet managers it has one tab, **Trips**. The **KPIs**, **Issues**, and **Searches** tabs aren't shown, and neither are the ride map and the fleet filter that admins see.

The list covers rides in the community shown on your sidebar card. It refreshes by itself: every 10 seconds while a ride is in progress, and every 60 seconds otherwise. The text next to **Refresh** shows how long ago it updated.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **All**, **Active**, **Completed**, **Canceled** | Filters the list by ride status. |
| **Search rider, driver or ride ID...** | Searches the list. |
| **Columns** | Shows or hides columns. **Rider** and **Status** always stay visible. |
| **Refresh** | Reloads the list now. |
| **Show N older rides (24hr+)** | The list starts with rides requested in the last 24 hours. Click this row to add older rides on the current page, and **Hide older rides** to remove them. |
| **Previous**, **Next** | Moves between pages of 50 rides. |

The status filter and the older rides setting are saved in the page URL, so you can bookmark a filtered list.

The columns are **Rider**, **Driver**, **Status**, **When**, **TTM**, **TTP**, **TTD**, **Wait**, **Time**, **Distance**, **Fare**, **Pickup**, **Dropoff**, **Fleet**, and **Rating**. Hover over a timing column heading to see what it measures. A ride that's confirmed but has no driver yet shows **Awaiting match** in the **Driver** column. For every column, see [Trips columns](/dashboard/rides#trips-columns).

Clicking a ride row doesn't open ride details. That panel is for admins.

<Note>
  Search offers **Ride KPIs**, **Ride issues**, and **Ride searches** results to fleet managers. All three open the **Trips** tab.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Fleet page says &quot;You are not assigned to a fleet.&quot;">
    Your account has no valid fleet assignment. Ask a Yelo admin to add one on the **Access** page. See [Missing or malformed assignments](/administration/dashboard-roles#missing-or-malformed-assignments).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A sidebar item is dimmed">
    The page isn't available to fleet managers. You can open **Fleet**, **Drivers**, and **Rides**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't find my other community">
    The sidebar card has no menu. Open search, type the community name, and select it under **Communities**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The fleet dropdown on the Fleet page doesn't open">
    The dropdown is disabled when you have only one fleet in the selected community. If your other fleet is in a different community, switch communities with search.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
