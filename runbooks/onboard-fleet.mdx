---
title: "Onboard a fleet"
description: "Checklist for setting up a new fleet: payment model, creation, Stripe onboarding, service communities, pricing, vehicles, manager access, drivers, and readiness checks"
---

A fleet is ready when it's active, serves its communities, has prices riders can see, has drivers online under it, and, for fleet-managed payouts, has a complete Stripe account. This checklist gets a fleet to that state. All steps need the `admin` role unless noted.

Read [Fleets](/concepts/fleets) first, especially [Payment models](/concepts/fleets#payment-models).

## Before you start

Decide the **Payment Model**. You can't change it after you create the fleet.

| Payment model | Who gets paid | Fleet state at creation | Payout-ready when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner Operator** | Each driver, through their own Stripe account | Active, Stripe status `not_required` | Not applicable. Each driver completes their own Stripe onboarding |
| **Fleet Merchant** | The fleet's Stripe account | **Inactive** until Stripe onboarding completes | Active, **Custom Payouts** on, and Stripe status `complete` |

<Warning>
A **Fleet Merchant** fleet needs **Custom Payouts** on to be payout-ready. An **Owner Operator** fleet with **Custom Payouts** on can't accept driver joins at all.
</Warning>

## Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the fleet">
    In **Fleets** (Configuration group), select **Create Fleet**. Pick the home **Community**, then fill in **Name**, **Payment Model**, **Supported Vehicle Types**, **Primary Vehicle Type**, **Custom Payouts**, and **Require rider confirmation code**. Select **Create Fleet**. See [Create a fleet](/dashboard/fleets#create-a-fleet).

    Creating a fleet also:

    - Creates the fleet's service row for its home community.
    - Copies pricing. By default, for each supported vehicle type it copies one active price row from another fleet in the community. With **Import from Existing Fleet**, it shares the source fleet's Stripe account and, with **Copy Pricing from Source**, copies the source fleet's prices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish Stripe onboarding (Fleet Merchant only)">
    Copy or open the **Stripe Onboarding Link** from the banner and send it to the fleet's owner. The link expires in about 24 hours. Generate a new one with **Refresh Link** on the **Operations** tab.

    When Stripe reports the account complete, Yelo sets the fleet's Stripe status to `complete` and activates the fleet. If the **Stripe Account** card shows everything enabled but the stored status hasn't changed, see [Payout issues](/runbooks/payout-issues#fix).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set service communities">
    On the **Operations** tab, open **Service communities** and add any other community the fleet serves. See [Manage service communities](/dashboard/fleets#manage-service-communities). A fleet's prices only reach riders in a community while the service is current, the fleet is active, and the community is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review pricing">
    On **Pricing Management**, check the copied configs for each service community. Copies don't carry **Always shown**, so turn it on where riders should see the fleet's option before any driver is online. Add configs for communities that have none. See [Ride pricing](/dashboard/ride-pricing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add vehicles">
    Add fleet-owned vehicles on the fleet editor's **Vehicles** tab, or let owner-operators add their own. See [Fleet vehicles](/dashboard/fleet-vehicles). A driver can only go online with a vehicle from their active fleet or a personal vehicle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the fleet manager access">
    On **Access**, invite the manager as a fleet manager and assign the fleet and community. See [Invite a user](/dashboard/access-and-settings#invite-a-user) and [Fleet managers](/dashboard/access-and-settings#fleet-managers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in drivers">
    - **Invitation link.** On **Invite drivers**, create the fleet's reusable link and share it. It's only available when the fleet can accept joins. See [Fleet driver invitations](/fleets/driver-invitations).
    - **Existing driver.** On the driver's user detail sheet, add the fleet in **Fleet membership** and set it active, or use **Assign active payout fleet**, which only lists payout-ready fleets. Both are admin-only.
    - **Community default.** A community's **Primary Fleet** no longer enrolls drivers. Only the default community's primary fleet receives drivers who have no fleet, and it must be an active **Owner Operator** fleet without custom payouts.

    Fleet managers can't enroll drivers directly. `POST /dashboard/fleet/{fleet_id}/drivers` returns 403 for them, so they share the join link.

    Drivers still need an activated application. See [Driver can't go online](/runbooks/driver-cannot-go-online).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: hours, QR payments, and the PIN">
    - Attach the fleet's Google Calendar for operating hours. See [Fleet operating hours](/admin/operating-hours).
    - Create QR payment links for street pickups and tips. See [QR payments](/dashboard/qr-payments).
    - **Require rider confirmation code** is on by default. Turn it off if drivers should start rides with a single tap.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify

**Read-only:**

```sql
BEGIN TRANSACTION READ ONLY;

SELECT name, is_active, deleted_at, payment_model, has_custom_payouts,
       stripe_status, supported_vehicle_types, requires_confirmation_code
FROM "Fleets" WHERE id = '<fleet-id>';

SELECT community_id, enabled, origin, retired_at
FROM "FleetCommunityServices" WHERE fleet_id = '<fleet-id>';

SELECT community_id, vehicle_type, pricing_type, is_featured
FROM "RidePriceParameters" WHERE fleet_id = '<fleet-id>' AND end_time IS NULL;

SELECT fd.driver_id, fd.is_active
FROM "FleetDrivers" fd WHERE fd.fleet_id = '<fleet-id>';

ROLLBACK;
```

- The fleet is active and not archived (`deleted_at` is null). For **Fleet Merchant**, `has_custom_payouts` is true and `stripe_status` is `complete`.
- Each service community has `enabled = true`.
- Each service community has open price rows for the fleet's vehicle types.
- The fleet appears under **Select a payout-ready fleet** on a user detail sheet (**Fleet Merchant** only).
- The invitation card can create a link.
- A test driver joins, goes online under the fleet, and sees board rides priced for this fleet.

## Common gaps

| Missed step | Symptom |
| --- | --- |
| Stripe onboarding not finished | Fleet stays inactive, prices don't reach riders, and **Invitations unavailable**. |
| **Custom Payouts** off on a **Fleet Merchant** fleet | Not payout-ready. Drivers see **Fleet Payouts Unavailable** when selecting it. |
| **Custom Payouts** on for **Owner Operator** | Joins are refused. |
| No **Always shown** pricing | The fleet's option only appears while one of its drivers is online with auto-queue on. |
| Service community retired | The fleet's prices disappear in that community. |

## Archive a fleet

**Archive fleet** on the fleet's page under **Fleets** (Configuration) calls `DELETE /dashboard/communities/{id}/fleets/{fleet_id}`. It sets `is_active = false` and `deleted_at`, clears `driver_join_code`, and keeps the fleet row, its rides and payments, and its Stripe account reference. It refuses with "Cannot archive fleet: …" until all of these are resolved:

| Blocker | Resolve with |
| --- | --- |
| Drivers who have this fleet active | Switch them in **Fleet membership** |
| `DriverApplications.payout_fleet_id` pointing at the fleet | Move the driver's payout fleet |
| Open `DriverSessions` | The driver goes offline |
| Rides not in `complete`, `pending_rider_rating`, or `no_drivers_available` | Let them finish, or see [Stuck or ghost ride](/runbooks/stuck-ride) |
| A community with this fleet as **Primary Fleet** | Pick another **Primary Fleet** |
| Unarchived fleet vehicles, or fleet vehicles a user has selected | Archive or reassign the vehicles |
| Active QR payment links | Deactivate them. A Stripe failure blocks deactivation |

Archiving also deactivates whitelist entries, retires the fleet's service communities, and ends current and future pricing. Inactive memberships stay visible as **Archived** and can be removed. Database triggers from migration `000346` reject new memberships, rides, sessions, vehicles, payout assignments, QR links, and **Primary Fleet** references to an archived fleet.
