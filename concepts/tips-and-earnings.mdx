Drivers earn money three ways on Yelo:

- **Ride fares**, captured from the rider's payment. See [Payments and payouts](/concepts/payments-and-payouts).
- **In-app ride tips**, left when the rider rates a completed trip.
- **QR tip links**, paid through Stripe Checkout without a ride. Anyone can scan one, including people without a Yelo account.

All three land in the same connected Stripe account: the driver's own, or their fleet's when the fleet manages payouts. The earnings API then reads that account to show balances and run payouts.

## In-app ride tips

### When tips are enabled

Tips are enabled per ride. The mobile app sends `X-Features: tips` when it creates the ride, and the API stores `tips_enabled = true` on the row (`internal/utilities/features`). Rides created without that header never offer tips.

For a tip-enabled paid ride:

- **At accept**: the PaymentIntent authorizes the fare plus a **$5.00 tip buffer** (`models.TipBuffer`).
- **At complete**: capture is deferred and `tip_window_expires_at` is set to **24 hours** after completion. The ride moves to `pending_rider_rating`.

### Tip options

`GET /ride/i/{id}/tip-options` returns the tip choices for the rider's ride while it's in `pending_rider_rating` with tips enabled:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `tip_options` | `[2, 5, 10]` dollars (`models.GetTipOptions`) |
| `max_tip` | `100` dollars (`models.MaxTip`) |
| `fare` | The ride's effective fare |
| `currency` | `usd` |

Custom tips are accepted between $0 and $100.00 and validated server-side.

### Charging the tip

The rider tips by rating: `POST /ride/i/{id}/rate` with an optional `tip_amount`. `RideService.captureTip` (`internal/service/ride/tip_charge.go`) picks the cheapest correct charge:

<Steps>
  <Step title="No tip">
    If the ride is still pending, the fare is captured and the ride completes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tip within the buffer">
    For a tip of $5.00 or less on a pending paid ride, one capture of fare + tip runs on the original PaymentIntent. The ride's `tip_amount` is set and `driver_payout` becomes fare + tip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tip over the buffer, or a free ride">
    The fare is captured first, which releases the buffer. Then a separate **tip PaymentIntent** is created on the same connected account: automatic capture, off-session, using the rider's cloned payment method. For free rides, the payment method is cloned to the matched driver's or fleet's account at this point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authentication required">
    If the card needs authentication, the rate call returns `tip_status: requires_action` with `tip_client_secret` and `tip_connected_account_id`. The API exposes `POST /ride/i/{id}/confirm-tip` for a client to call after authenticating the card, but the mobile app currently ignores `requires_action` and never calls it, so the tip is not recorded. When called, the API retrieves the stored tip PaymentIntent from Stripe and records the tip only if the status is `succeeded`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The rate response includes `tip_status`: `none`, `captured`, or `requires_action`. See [Ride tips](/engineering/payments/ride-tips).

Safeguards:

- A ride that already has a tip is never charged again.
- Tip PaymentIntents use the idempotency key `tip_{rideId}_{cents}`. A retried request doesn't double-charge, and a changed amount creates a fresh intent after canceling the old one.
- If the tip-window sweeper settles the fare at the same moment a within-buffer tip arrives, the tip falls back to a dedicated tip charge instead of being lost.
- A driver or fleet with no connected account can't receive tips, and the rider sees "This driver can't accept tips right now."

### Tip-window sweeper

`RideService.AutoCaptureTipWindowExpired` (`tip_capture.go`) runs every 5 minutes in the `TipCapture` background loop. Each run:

1. Loads up to 50 rides that are `pending_rider_rating`, tip-enabled, have a PaymentIntent, and whose `tip_window_expires_at` has passed, oldest first.
2. Captures the **fare only**, which releases the $5.00 buffer.
3. Sets `tip_amount = 0`, `driver_payout = fare`, and `status = 'complete'`, but only if the ride is still pending. This lets a concurrent rating win cleanly.
4. Records a `tip_window_expired` ride event.

Failures are retried on the next run. A ride still stuck more than an hour past its window is logged as needing manual review.

## QR tip links

QR tip links let someone tip without a ride. They're served by the **tipping** service (`internal/service/tipping`) and the web tip page at `joinyelo.com/pay/tip/{slug}` (the signup site in `yelo-web`). The mobile app has a matching `tip/[slug]` route.

A slug resolves to one of two targets:

| Target | How it resolves | Amount rules | Paid to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **QR payment config** | An active config in the dashboard's QR payments section, matched by slug | The config's `fixed` amount, or its `custom` min and max | The config's fleet account. The fleet must have a Stripe account. |
| **Driver link** | A driver's referral code. The driver must have a complete application and not be banned or deleted. If they're in a fleet, it must be active. | Custom, $1.00 to $500.00, preset $5.00 | The fleet's account when the fleet has custom payouts, otherwise the driver's own account |

Every tip must be at least $0.50.

Endpoints live under `/tips`:

| Endpoint | Auth | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /tips/{slug}` | Public | Tip page config: names, driver photo, amount rules. |
| `POST /tips/{slug}/guest-checkout` | Public | Creates a Stripe Checkout session for a guest. |
| `POST /tips/{slug}/visit` | JWT | Records a visit and sets the user's acquisition source to QR payment if it's still the default. |
| `POST /tips/{slug}/checkout` | JWT | Creates a Checkout session with the user's email, name, and phone attached. |

Checkout sessions are created on the connected account. Metadata includes `recipient_driver_id`, `fleet_id`, `qr_config_id`, and `yelo_user_id` when available. When Stripe sends `checkout.session.completed`, `PaymentService.CheckoutSessionCompleted` writes a `QrPayments` row with the amount, customer details, and fleet and driver attribution.

## Driver earnings

The earnings hub in the driver app (`yelo-frontend/app/(app)/driver-earnings/`) is backed by `/earnings`. Every endpoint resolves the signed-in user's own `Users.stripe_merchant_id` and calls Stripe on that account. If the driver has no connected account, the API returns "Finish setting up your driver payouts before viewing earnings."

<Note>
Earnings reads only the driver's own connected account. Fares and tips for drivers paid through a fleet with custom payouts land in the fleet's account, so they don't appear in that driver's balance or payouts.
</Note>

| Endpoint | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /earnings/balance` | Available, pending, and instantly available balance. |
| `GET /earnings/external-account` | The default bank account or debit card, and whether it supports instant payouts. Returns `404` when none is set. |
| `GET /earnings/payout-schedule` | Current cadence and next expected payout date. |
| `PUT /earnings/payout-schedule` | Set `daily`, `weekly`, `monthly`, or `manual`. Weekly defaults to Friday. |
| `GET /earnings/payouts` | Past payouts, newest first. 25 per page by default, 100 max. |
| `GET /earnings/payouts/instant-quote` | Instant balance, fee, net amount, and eligibility. |
| `POST /earnings/payouts/instant` | Cash out instantly. An amount of `0`, or more than available, sends the full instant balance. |
| `GET /earnings/activity` | Balance transactions grouped by day, with `from`, `to`, `limit`, and `cursor`. |

### Instant payouts

- **Fee**: 1% of the amount, rounded up, with a $0.50 minimum, and never more than the amount (`instantFeeFor` in `internal/service/earnings/mappers.go`).
- **Ineligible when** there's no external account, the bank doesn't support instant payouts, there's no instant balance, or the balance is $0.50 or less.
- Payout requests carry an idempotency key built from the account, method, amount, and a time bucket, so a double tap doesn't send two payouts.

### Activity feed

`GET /earnings/activity` lists Stripe balance transactions, classified as charge, payment, payout, refund, adjustment, fee, or other. Each charge row is matched to its ride through the PaymentIntent. When a match is found, the row gets the rider's name, the tip amount, the ride ID, and the gross amount from `driver_payout`.

On the first page, the "earned this week" headline and ride count come from the database, not Stripe. They sum `driver_payout` for the driver's `complete` and `pending_rider_rating` rides since Monday 00:00 UTC. Stripe books the full pre-authorization on partial-capture rides, so its totals would overstate earnings.

### Payout notifications

Stripe `payout.paid`, `payout.failed`, and `payout.canceled` events for a connected account trigger `EarningsService.HandlePayoutEvent`. It finds the driver who owns the account and sends a push that deep-links to `yelo://drive/earnings`, deduplicated by event ID. Payout events on the platform account, and accounts with no matching driver, are ignored.
